## Context

Filed 2026-10-28 from the user's decision on EPIC-10 Q-1: *"leave it as
drawn, but file a followup issue for v2 that does remove external watermarks
(external as in not associated with the manga or its artist or publisher)"*.

v1 (EPIC-10, MT-118 to MT-121) **leaves an excluded region as drawn**: it is
not sent to the model, not inpainted and not typeset over (EPIC-10 PO-5).
That is safe, but the finished page still carries the scanlation site's mark.
v2 erases it.

**"External" is the whole difficulty.** An external watermark is one added by
someone other than the manga's artist or publisher: a scan or aggregator
site's name or URL stamped on the art, e.g. `RawLazy.Com` and `DL-Raw.Se`
(MT-007 A-4, `spikes/MT-002/pages/011-015.jpg`). It is **not** any of these,
which must never be erased:

- the artist's signature or the publisher's or magazine's logo, copyright line
  or credit;
- a chapter title or title card (e.g. `第134話 神の提案`);
- sound effects drawn as part of the art;
- narration or caption boxes (MT-117 measures whether the detector's class 0
  ever covers these).

So "excluded" is not enough to erase: a region excluded by the user may be any
of the above. Erasing needs its own, narrower signal that a region is external.

**What v1 leaves behind that this story must fix first.** The detector's box
for the watermark the user saw clipped its first and last letters (the user's
screenshot of 2026-10-28, page 1 of the `mato sample` chapter: `awLazy.Co`
inside the box, `R` and `m` outside). v1 does not care - an excluded box is
never used. Erasing does: inpainting a clipped box leaves the outer letters
standing. The erase area has to cover the whole mark.

## Acceptance criteria

Not yet written. A parked story is cut to criteria when it is un-parked,
against what MT-117 found and what v1 actually shipped. The behaviour it must
reach, for whoever writes them:

- an external watermark region is erased (inpainted) on the cleaned and baked
  page, with no residual letters at its ends;
- nothing that is not an external watermark - the list above - is erased by
  this rule, measured on real pages with a decision rule written first, as
  MT-117 does;
- the user can see that a region was erased as a watermark and can undo it per
  region, as MT-120 lets them restore an excluded one;
- a region the user excluded by hand is left as drawn, as in v1, unless they
  ask for it to be erased.

## Out of scope

- Anything about the artist's or publisher's own marks, which are never
  erased.
- Translating watermark text.
