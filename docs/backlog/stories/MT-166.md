## Context

EPIC-13, after its closing audit. `docs/wiki/audits/MT-159-epic-13-done-when.md` (`## Decided`, lines 1 and 2;
`## Evidence` E-3 and E-4) found that the epic's first two "Done when" lines
are not met on the development set, and that fragment pieces are the reason
for both:

- **Line 1, `spurious`.** 19 fragment pieces are still spurious. With every
  art-text region taken off, the fall would be 87 against the 106 the line
  asks for, so the pieces alone decide it.
- **Line 2, `recall`.** 12 of the 16 balloons MT-143 counted as missed
  because fragmented are still missed. `matched` rose by 4 on the no-spend
  run, against 16.

The twelve, by truth index, each with its pieces by reading index (audit
E-3, no-spend project `D:\bench\blackjack-v1-ep02-03-mt159.mtproj`):

| Truth balloon | Pieces | Truth balloon | Pieces |
|---|---|---|---|
| p063 t4 | r4 | p084 t0 | r0 |
| p064 t0 | r0 | p084 t1 | r1, r2 |
| p065 t1 | r1, r3 | p099 t6 | r6 |
| p077 t0 | r0 | p104 t0 | r0, r1 |
| p082 t1 | r1, r2 | p105 t0 | r0, r3 |
| p082 t2 | r3 | p105 t1 | r1, r2, r4 |

**Why this is a spike and not a fix.** The rule that would join them is not
known, so a fix could not state its criteria or name its files:

- MT-145 swept both gap limits of `detect/columns.py` over their safe bands
  on these same pages. No setting left fewer pieces than the shipped one
  without joining two user-confirmed regions (MT-145 `## Notes`, "GREEN: the
  constants"). So the answer is not a different value of
  `COLUMN_MAX_GAP_PX` or `COLUMN_MAX_RUN_GAP_PX`.
- Six of the twelve hold exactly **one** piece (p063 t4, p064 t0, p077 t0,
  p082 t2, p084 t0, p099 t6). A piece is a region lying at least half inside
  a truth balloon and matching it at IoU under 0.5. With one piece there is
  no second region to merge it with, so a merge rule cannot be what these
  six lack. What the rest of each truth polygon holds was not looked at.
- Some of the rest are split by a decision already made. p104's two columns
  sit 38 px apart and stay split by choice (`columns.py`,
  `COLUMN_MAX_GAP_PX`). p082 t1's two pieces are the ends of one long dash
  (MT-145 measured a run gap of 116; MT-168 owns the ink left between
  them). Balloons drawn joined stay separate by the user's decision of
  2026-11-11 (EPIC-13 PO-1, amended).

**What is settled.** The twelve balloons, their 19 pieces and the method
that classes them (MT-143's, restated in the audit's E-3). The user's
decisions above. That no threshold is derived from the benchmark chapter.

**What is evidence, to verify.** Every count above is one run on one
machine, classed by a script that is new and unreviewed (audit E-3, "How it
could be wrong"). This story reproduces the twelve before it explains them.
The gap figures quoted from MT-145 were measured on raw regions before
MT-145's rule existed, and are re-measured here.

**Output.** One audit, `docs/wiki/audits/MT-166-fragmented-balloons.md`, in
the shape of `docs/wiki/audits/TEMPLATE.md`. The story changes no source
and no test, so no gate reads its artifact; `check-boundaries.sh` reads the
commit. Scratch code stays outside the repository, as MT-159's did.

## Acceptance criteria

"The development set" is MT-143's 46 pages (p061 to p108) and its truth
document, sha256
`68161def30c014abd0be260e29b5fa33225a1e125c70adc47a514878c942bc10`. "Raw
regions" are the detector's regions before `merge_columns` runs. Gaps and
overlaps are in `detect/columns.py`'s half-open convention. A region is
named by its page file and its index, and the audit says each time whether
the index is the truth document's (`t`) or the reading index (`r`).

- **AC-1** - Given detect run on a fresh copy of the development set at a
  commit whose `src/` and `packaging/` equal `origin/main`'s, when the audit
  is read, then it records the commit, the folder, the truth document's
  sha256, and the fragmented balloons and their pieces as classed by
  MT-143's method.
  *Control:* the count is 12 balloons and 19 pieces, and they are the twelve
  and the 19 named in this story's `## Context`. If the run gives anything
  else, the audit pastes the difference and explains the balloons it found,
  and does not present its findings as an explanation of MT-159's twelve.
- **AC-2** - Given the twelve balloons, when the audit is read, then a table
  has one row per balloon giving its cause, from the evidence named here:
  - for a balloon with **one** piece: the share of the truth polygon's
    raster the piece's mask covers, what the uncovered part holds (text no
    raw region covers, text held by another region and which, or no text),
    and the source crop that was looked at;
  - for a balloon with **two or more** pieces: for each pair of its raw
    regions, the fence box each belongs to, the gap across the run, the gap
    along the run, both overlaps, and which clause of the rule refuses the
    join.

  A cause the evidence does not show is written `cause not known`, with
  what was measured.
  *Control:* the same measuring script, on the raw regions of a balloon the
  shipped rule does join (p061 t0, whose region carries `merged_from` 0 to
  4), reports every link the rule made as passing. If it reports a refusal
  there, the script is wrong and no row is written from it.
- **AC-3** - Given the causes of AC-2, when the audit is read, then for each
  cause it either states a candidate rule precisely enough to run again, or
  says that no rule inside `merge_columns` can address it and why. For each
  candidate rule it gives, measured on all 46 pages:
  - how many of the twelve come back as one region matching their truth
    balloon at IoU 0.5 or more;
  - the number of wrong joins, counted as MT-145 did: merged groups with
    members in two user-confirmed truth regions;
  - for each of MT-145's named pairs (p075 2/3, p063 4/5, p089 5/6, p095
    6/7, p098 0/1, p104 1/2), whether it stays separate.

  *Control:* the wrong-join counter, run with `COLUMN_MAX_GAP_PX` at 54,
  reports p063 4/5 and p089 5/6 as joined (MT-145 `## Notes`). A counter
  that reports zero there cannot see a wrong join, and no candidate's
  "zero wrong joins" is written from it.
- **AC-4** - Given AC-2 and AC-3, when the audit's `## Decided` is read,
  then each of the twelve balloons carries exactly one of: `joinable`,
  naming the rule and its wrong-join count of zero; `not joinable by a merge
  rule`, naming what it would take instead; or `the user's decision`, naming
  the decision that keeps it split. The section also gives what EPIC-13's
  lines 1 and 2 would read if every `joinable` balloon were joined, as
  arithmetic on the audit's own counts, labelled as a prediction.
- **AC-5** - Given the audit, when it is read, then each follow-up it
  proposes is a title with the files it would write, and none is filed
  without the user. If it proposes none, it says so and says which of the
  epic's two lines therefore stay not met.
- **AC-6** - Given the whole story, when its diff and its audit are read,
  then no page of the benchmark chapter (pages 3 to 58) was read or run, no
  figure in the audit was measured on it, no paid call was made, and `git
  diff --quiet origin/main HEAD -- src packaging tests` exits 0.
  *Control:* `grep -nE 'p0(0[3-9]|[1-4][0-9]|5[0-8])'` over the audit prints
  nothing, or only lines labelled as quoted from the epic or an earlier
  audit.

## Out of scope

- The benchmark chapter, in any form, and any paid call.

  amended 2026-11-11); the audit names them and does not propose joining
  them.

  `BOX_CONFIDENCE` or NMS (EPIC-13, "Deliberately not in this epic"). If a
  cause is "the detector did not find the text", the audit says so and
  stops there.
- The three truth regions missed with no piece at all (p062 t2, p087 t2,
  p096 t1). They were missed before the epic and are not fragmented.
