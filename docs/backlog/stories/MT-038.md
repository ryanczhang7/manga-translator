## Context

**Split out of MT-011 by a user decision on 2026-09-18** (MT-011 PO-1). MT-011
originally carried this as its AC-8. Every other criterion in MT-011 is
mechanical and runs against recorded fixtures under the `unit` and `coverage`
gates, both required; this one needs a real, paid API call and therefore a
credential that did not exist on the development machine. Carrying it inside
MT-011 meant one criterion that no required gate could discharge, in a story
where seven others could — and the predictable outcome of that shape is a
waiver, which is how the epic's one required measured number quietly never gets
measured.

`EPIC-04`'s "Done when it is *not* done" section is explicit:

> An epic that reports "cost looks fine" without a measured number has not
> finished.

That sentence is this story. `docs/wiki/stack.md` §5/O4 puts the chosen design
at **$1.14 per 20-page chapter** against a **$2.00** ceiling, and
`docs/wiki/cost-model.awk` shows the chapter crossing the ceiling at **3,200
adaptive-thinking tokens per page** against the assumed **800**. The estimate is
a guess in exactly one respect and this is the story that replaces it with a
number.

## Acceptance criteria

*Draft — to be confirmed and made testable in this story's own PLANNED phase.*

- **AC-1** — Given a real API call for one fixture page, when it completes, then
  `response.usage` is recorded, and this story's `## Notes` carries the
  **measured** input, output, cache-read and cache-write token counts alongside
  the estimates in `docs/wiki/stack.md` §5/O4.
- **AC-2** — Given a second page sent after the first within the cache TTL, when
  it completes, then `usage.cache_read_input_tokens` is **greater than zero** —
  which is what makes the cost model's "19 cache reads per 20-page chapter"
  assumption a measurement rather than a hope. See the risk below: this is the
  criterion most likely to fail, and failing it is a *finding*, not a defect.
- **AC-3** — Given the measured figures, when `docs/wiki/cost-model.awk` is
  re-run with them in place of its assumptions, then the resulting per-chapter
  figure is recorded in `stack.md` §5/O4, and a figure above $2.00 is reported to
  the user as a breach of a stated constraint rather than absorbed.

## Known risk this story exists to measure

**The stable prefix may be too short to cache at all.** Prompt caching has a
model-dependent **minimum cacheable prefix** (512–4096 tokens), and a prefix
below that minimum **silently does not cache** — no error, no warning, and
`cache_read_input_tokens` simply stays at zero. `stack.md` §5/O4 estimates the
stable prefix (system prompt, lettering style guide, glossary header) at
**≈1,500 tokens**. If Opus 5's minimum is 2,048 or 4,096, the cost model's
caching assumption is worth nothing and the chapter costs more than $1.14.

This was identified during MT-011's PLANNED phase from the Anthropic API
reference and is **unverified**. AC-2 is the measurement. If it comes back zero,
the fix is a product decision (pad the stable prefix to clear the minimum, or
re-price the chapter without caching) and goes to the user.

## Deferred verifications

- *Falsifiable condition 1:* with the `cache_control` breakpoint moved **after**
