## Context

Filed 2026-10-17 at MT-075's PLANNED → RED (its PO-1): EPIC-07's done-when
says **`bench`** — one command — "can be pointed at a benchmark chapter plus
its ground truth and prints S1b, S1a, recall, spurious, S2 and S5". After
MT-075 all six numbers exist, across three commands: `python -m
mangatl.bench.acceptance` (MT-022), `python -m mangatl.bench.s2` (MT-023) and
`python -m mangatl.bench.cost` (MT-075). MT-075's stub deferred the combined
command to "MT-024's console-script decision", and MT-024 closed without one.

This story is the glue and nothing else: `python -m mangatl.bench FOLDER
[--truth PATH]` runs the three measurements in order against one opened
project and one loaded document, prints their lines in one report, and exits
as they do (`0` measured, `2` cannot measure — and S1/S2's development-use
refusal refuses the whole report, S5 included). Whether it also becomes a
console script (`mangatl-bench` in `pyproject.toml`) is the PO decision to
take at its own PLANNED → RED, against what the installer (MT-024) ships.

## Acceptance criteria

*To be written at this story's own PLANNED → RED. Expected shape:*

- **AC-1** — Given a chapter with a valid ground truth and a ledger, when
  `python -m mangatl.bench FOLDER` runs, then stdout carries S1b, S1a, recall,
  spurious, S2's verdict and S5's chapter cost, each the same line its own
  command prints, and exits `0`.
- **AC-2** — Given a document flagged `development_use`, when it runs, then it
  prints nothing to stdout, one refusal to stderr, and exits `2` — S5 is not
  printed for a chapter the harness refuses.
- **AC-3** — Each measurement is called through its module's public function,
  not re-implemented: a source check pins the three imports.
