## Context

EPIC-13, following MT-145 (its `## Diff review`, findings 1 and 2).
`src/mangatl/detect/columns.py` merges regions of one fence box when a
**pair** is side by side (gap across the run at most `COLUMN_MAX_GAP_PX` = 26)
or stacked (gap along the run at most `COLUMN_MAX_RUN_GAP_PX` = 25), each with
an overlap of at least `COLUMN_MIN_OVERLAP` = 0.5 of the **smaller** extent,
and then closes that relation transitively (`_components`). A small piece
overlaps fully by construction, so it can carry a join between two regions no
pair rule joins. Two 40x200 columns 42 px apart in one fence stay apart; put a
10x10 piece between them (gaps 16 and 16) and all three come back as one
region. At the old limit of 12 they did not. The same holds stacked: two
pieces 40 px apart join through a 10x10 piece with gaps 15 and 15. No test
covers either, and the module docstring reads as pairwise. In the app the
result is two balloons in one review line with one translation.

Finding 2 rides along: `COLUMN_MIN_OVERLAP`'s comment describes only its
side-by-side use. The stacked clause uses the same value across the run,
against the narrower piece, and its only measured negative is p075's 0.156.

**OPEN QUESTION D-1 - a product call, not yet made. This story must not leave
PLANNED until the user answers it and the criteria marked PENDING are
rewritten to the one variant chosen.** Measured on the development set
(`## Notes`, "Measurement"):

- No small piece joins two different user-confirmed regions today (0 of 111
  bridge-shaped links), and the 7 fence boxes that hold two confirmed regions
  hold no stray region at all. The defect is reachable and has not been
  observed.
- Joining through a middle piece is how real balloons hold together: 107 such
  links join pieces of one confirmed region, in 18 regions. A rule that
  refused every one of them would split those 18.
- A narrower refusing rule was prototyped in scratch: a piece that reaches a
  partner only because it is the smaller side joins **one** partner, the
  nearest. It splits no confirmed region and leaves the fragment counts where
  they are (bubble 10, box 9, wrong joins 0). It changes one group: the sign
  `第一外科` on p089 (art text, outside the truth), where the flat `一` is
  the bridge, comes back as `第一` and `外科` (270 regions become 271).
- Small-middle bridges under half size occur twice on the 46 pages, and both
  are correct joins (p089's `一`; p062's short column piece, which the
  prototype keeps only because a second piece also links its neighbours).

The two options:

- **Option A - pin it.** Keep the rule. Tests pin that a piece of any size
  carries the join, the docstring says so and says why, and the measurement
  is recorded. No behaviour changes. Cost: the fuse stays reachable by a stray
  mark between two balloons in one fence.
- **Option B - refuse it** with the one-partner rule in `## Contract`. Cost: a
  second linking concept in the rule; one real word split on the development
  set (p089's sign); thin glyphs detected as their own piece in the middle of
  a group (a long-vowel bar, an ellipsis) are exposed to the same split, seen
  once (p062) and rescued there by a second link.

The planner's recommendation is **Option A**: nothing measured shows the harm,
and both measured small bridges are joins the user would want. The user
decided in MT-145 that joined balloons stay separate, which is the argument
for B, and why this is theirs to call.

Required gates: `unit`, `coverage` (both `required`, both read
`src/mangatl/**`).

```python
def _components(extents, fenced, axis) -> list[list[int]]: ...   # signature unchanged
```

- A **link** is a pair in one fence box that passes `_is_one_utterance`, as
  today.
- A link is **mutual** when, for at least one clause that holds, the overlap
  is also at least `COLUMN_MIN_OVERLAP` of the **larger** extent on that
  clause's overlap axis (the taller for side by side, the wider for stacked).
  Otherwise it is **one-sided**, and its **dependent** is the member with the
  smaller extent on the overlap axis of the clause that holds (the
  side-by-side clause when both hold).
- Mutual links are closed transitively, exactly as today.
- A region that is the dependent of one or more one-sided links joins exactly
  **one** of those partners: the smallest gap of the clause that holds, ties
  to the partner whose oriented extent sorts first as `(y0, x0, y1, x1)`. The
  tie-break is geometric so that the answer never depends on input order.
- No new constant: the larger-extent test reuses `COLUMN_MIN_OVERLAP`. On the
  development set the result is the same at 0.5, 0.33 and 0.25.
- `merged_from`, the union mask, the weighted confidence and the output order
  are untouched.
- Known limit, to be written in the docstring, not solved: two or more small
  pieces that are mutual with **each other** can still carry a join between
  two larger regions. Not observed on the development set.
- Predicted by the prototype, **to be verified, not assumed** (DV-2): the 62
