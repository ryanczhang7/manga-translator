## Context

EPIC-05 owns the review workspace and its theme. `components.md` §4
(`PageStrip`, lines 603-605 at origin/main f1b2c15) gives the current row a
**3px `color.accent.base` leading bar on a `color.surface.selected` ground**,
and §4 lines 634-640 record that none of the per-row states is a rule yet:
under the sheet the current row shows Qt's own item highlight, which is the
`selection-background-color` of the base `QWidget` rule (`theme.qss.in` 37).
That highlight is painted by `QListWidget::item` *under* the row widget
(`workspace.py` 482-488 sets each `_PageRow` with `setItemWidget`), so a
leading bar cannot be `::item` styling that reaches the row's labels, and
`_PageRow` (`workspace.py` 320) is a plain `QWidget` with no
`WA_StyledBackground` and no `paintEvent`, so a `#pageRow` rule today paints
nothing. Under High Contrast the problem is worse than "unbuilt": `[hc.map]`
(`tokens.toml` 302-355, `tokens_gen.py` 262-290) sends both
`color.surface.selected` and `color.accent.base` to `Highlight`, so the bar the
base rule would draw is Highlight on Highlight, invisible, and §10.2
(`components.md` 1261-1263 at f1b2c15) says the HC current row is `Highlight`
ground, `HighlightedText` text and a `HighlightedText` bar; today the glyph on
that row is `WindowText` on `Highlight`, a pair Windows does not guarantee.

This story builds the mechanism the Lead Designer chose on 2026-11-03 and the
user confirmed the same day: a `current` dynamic property on `_PageRow`, owned
and re-polished by `PageStrip`, with `WA_StyledBackground` so the row paints
its own QSS box (the `RunThumbnail` precedent, `run_thumbnail.py` 89); a base
rule and an HC override; and, in the 28 px rail, a constant left contents
margin of `border-width.bar` on every row so the glyph never sits under the
bar. It is the first of three (MT-134 → MT-135 → MT-136) and the one that
proves the mechanism; the two `TranslationRow` stories reuse it. Every line
number above was re-read in this worktree by the Lead PO at PLANNED, against
f1b2c15 plus the Lead Designer's uncommitted `components.md` amendments.

The required gate that fails if the artifact breaks: `unit` (and `coverage`,
the same suite instrumented) over `tests/ui`, which holds the paint test this
story adds and `test_tokens.py`'s template/sheet agreement checks.
`project.conf` `covers | unit | src/mangatl/**` reads `workspace.py`.

## Acceptance criteria

All sampling below is under the **packaged `theme.qss` set as the application
stylesheet** (`QApplication.setStyleSheet`, never `styleSheet()` on a widget),
in a shown `PageStrip` of at least three rows, with row *k* current
(`setCurrentRow(k)`), the rows fully inside the viewport. A row's "column *n*"
is the vertical line of pixels *n* pixels in from the row's left edge, over the
row's full height less `space.s1` pixels at the top and bottom (the strip's
item spacing band; RED measures the exact band and records it).

- **AC-1** — *The current row carries the bar.* Given the fixture, when row
  *k*'s columns are sampled, then columns 0, 1 and 2 are every pixel
  `color.accent.base` and column 3 is every pixel `color.surface.selected`.
- **AC-2** — *Control: a non-current row carries nothing.* Given the fixture,
  when every row other than *k* has its columns sampled, then columns 0 to 3
  are every pixel `color.surface.raised` (the list ground, `theme.qss.in` 170).
- **AC-3** — *Nothing moves.* Given the fixture with row *j ≠ k* current, when
  row *k* is made current, then every row's `geometry()` and `sizeHint()`, and
  every row's glyph and label `mapTo(viewport, (0, 0))`, are identical before
  and after, in both the 96 px strip and the 28 px rail.
- **AC-4** — *The bar is the contrast carrier, by the fixture table.* Given
  `theme.contrast_ratio` over `tokens_gen.TOKENS`, when the three pairs are
  computed, then `color.accent.base` on `color.surface.selected` ≥ 3:1 (5.31,
  `tokens.md` 193) and on `color.surface.raised` ≥ 3:1 (7.54, `tokens.md`
  194); *negative control:* `color.surface.selected` on `color.surface.raised`
  is **below** 3:1 (1.42, `accessibility.md` 38-41) — the reason a ground alone
  is not the carrier and the test that fails if someone "fixes" AC-1 with a
  ground.
- **AC-5** — *A selection change repaints.* Given row *k* current and sampled
  as AC-1, when row *k+1* is made current and both rows are re-sampled, then
  row *k+1* now satisfies AC-1 and row *k* satisfies AC-2. (Fails without the
  unpolish/polish on the property change: the sheet does not re-match on its
  own.)
- **AC-6** — *Under High Contrast the bar and the text are `HighlightedText`.*
  Given the sheet composed for High Contrast with the five-role test palette
  (`tests/ui/test_hc_error_banner_border.py` `PALETTE`) set as the application
  sheet, when row *k* is sampled, then columns 0 to 2 are every pixel
  `HighlightedText` and column 3 is every pixel `Highlight`; the ink of row
  *k*'s glyph label and index label is `HighlightedText`; and a non-current
  row's glyph ink is `WindowText` (control). *Negative control:* the **base
  template alone** rendered against the same palette (`test_theme_apply.py`
  `_render_base(True, PALETTE)` shape, no override appended) gives columns 0
  to 3 all `Highlight` — the invisible bar the override exists to fix.
- **AC-7** — *The override is a real override.* Given the composed HC sheet,
  when `tests/ui/test_theme_apply.py`'s `OVERRIDES` table is extended with
  the new selectors (C-3), then each one's effective last-wins rule carries
  the declared palette values and the last rule naming it is in the appended
  block (the two existing parametrised tests, two more cases each).

No AC names a statistic or a tuned threshold: AC-4's 3:1 is WCAG 1.4.11 as
`accessibility.md` fixes it, and its control is named. The central claim's
negative control is the defect put back (`## Deferred verifications`).

### C-1 The base rules (`docs/wiki/design/theme.qss.in`, new block after the `QLabel#pageGlyph` rules, 448-460)

Authored in this order, each restating only what it owns, under a
`/* components.md section 4 - PageStrip current row (MT-134) */` box comment
in the file's 76-column style:

```
#pageRow {
    background-color: transparent;
    border: none;
}

#pageRow[current="true"] {
    background-color: @{color.surface.selected};
    border-left: @{border-width.bar}px solid @{color.accent.base};
    color: @{color.text.primary};
}

#pageRow[current="true"] QLabel#pageGlyph,
#pageRow[current="true"] QLabel#pageLabel {
    background-color: transparent;
}
```

- The rest rule paints nothing: the list's `color.surface.raised`
  (`QAbstractItemView`, 170) shows through, and Qt's own `::item` highlight
  stays beneath the widget, hidden by the current row's ground. The `QWidget`
  rule's `selection-background-color` (37) is **kept**: it is the editors'
  text selection too.
- **The semantics of 3.** `border-width.bar = 3` (`tokens.toml` 207) is the
  width §4 and §10.2 give the leading bar, the same token MT-132/MT-133 use
  for the banner; `emphasis` (2) is a frame width, never a bar. The bar is
  drawn by QSS inside the widget's rect, over its leftmost 3 px: a border on a
  plain `QWidget` does not change its `sizeHint`, so no row moves (AC-3). If
  RED measures a reflow, the fallback is the primary button's
  (`theme.qss.in` 124-127): a constant `border-left: @{border-width.bar}px
  solid transparent` in the rest rule and `border-left-color` only in the
  state rule. RED amends this block if it needs that.
- `color: @{color.text.primary}` on the row is the base's declared text
  colour; QSS does **not** inherit `color` to children (each `QLabel` matches
  the `QWidget` rule itself), so it is honest but inert in the base. The two
  descendant selectors exist because `theme_hc.qss.in` may only restyle
  selectors the base defines verbatim (`check_override_template`,
  `tokens.py` 203-213; `test_tokens.py` 1336-1345); their base declaration is
  the label's real (transparent) ground, and deliberately **not** `color`,
  which at specificity (2,1,1) would beat the seven
  `QLabel#pageGlyph[status=...]` rules (1,1,1) and erase the status colours
  on the current row. Future slots in the same block, **not built here**:
  `[hovered]` ground, `[status="running"]` emphasis frame, focus ring on three
  sides (components.md 626-640, the designer's precedence list).

### C-4 `workspace.py`

- `_PageRow.__init__` (336): `self.setAttribute(Qt.WidgetAttribute.WA_StyledBackground, True)`
  and `self.setProperty("current", False)`.
- New `_PageRow.set_current(self, current: bool) -> None`: no-op when
  unchanged, else `setProperty("current", current)` then
  `self.style().unpolish(self); self.style().polish(self)` - the
  `TranslationRow.set_hovered` shape (784-790). The property is a `bool`; QSS
  matches `[current="true"]` against a bool property's string form, as
  `[hovered="true"]` would. RED pins the string `"true"` in the selector and
  the bool in the property.
  **RED amendment (2026-11-03, measured): `set_current` re-polishes the row's
  `glyph` and `label` too, not only the row.** Reason: a descendant selector
  (`#pageRow[current="true"] QLabel#pageGlyph`) is matched when *the label* is
  polished; re-polishing the parent does not re-match its children. Measured by
  simulating C-1/C-3/C-4 outside the suite (monkeypatched `_PageRow`, rules
  appended to the composed sheet), HC palette, row 1 current: with the row
  alone re-polished the glyph ink was `#1a2b3c` (Highlight - invisible on the
  Highlight ground) and the index ink `#fdfeff` (WindowText); with row, glyph
  and label re-polished both were `#c3b2a1` (HighlightedText). AC-6's ink
  assertion fails without it. (The bool property does match `[current="true"]`:
  confirmed in the same simulation.)
- **RED amendment (2026-11-03, measured): the item size hints must be honest
  before the first re-polish, or AC-3 fails.** Reason: `_resize_items` reads
  `row.sizeHint()` while the row's layout has not been activated, so today
  every item is 123 px tall at 96 px for a row whose real hint is 121.
  Re-polishing a row emits `FontChange` on it, so `fitChanged` ->
  `_refit_row` re-sizes *that* item to 121 and every row below it moves up 2
  px the first time it becomes current (simulated: rows 1..6 moved, both build
  orders - `set_pages` before or after `show()`; the rail is unaffected, its
  rows are 26 px either way). Activating each row's layout before reading its
  hint (`row.layout().activate()` in `_resize_items`) made AC-3's snapshot
  identical in the same simulation; the mechanism is GREEN's choice, the
  constraint is AC-3. This touches only `workspace.py`, already in `touches:`.
  *Lead PO reproduction (2026-11-03, independent probe, different inputs):*
  a fresh 96 px strip of five `pending` rows, offscreen, after `set_pages`
  and two event rounds - `item(i).sizeHint().height()` is **122** on rows
  0-2 while `itemWidget(i).sizeHint().height()` is **120**, before and after
  `layout().activate()`. The 2 px item-vs-row gap RED reports is real on a
  second row mix, so a refit on the first re-polish would move the rows
  below; the amendment stands.
- `_PageRow.set_rail` (360-375): in the rail the outer layout's left contents
  margin is `int(tokens_gen.TOKENS["border-width.bar"])` (3), the other three
  stay 0; the full strip keeps `SPACE_S3` on all four as today
  (`test_page_strip_thumbnails.py` 482-495 pins 12/12 at 96 px and is
  unchanged). `index_fits` (377-380) subtracts that margin from
  `STRIP_RAIL_WIDTH` so the hint stays honest: the rail font measures "99" at
  24 px and "100" at 36 px (`test_page_strip_rail_fit.py` 9-11), so the split
  at page 100 does not move (24 ≤ 25 < 36) and every rail row's hint stays
  ≤ 28 (`test_page_strip_status.py` 485). The docstrings at 377-379 and the
  test's line 7 say "zero contents margins"; GREEN rewords the source one,
  the test's is a comment and stays.
- `PageStrip`: owns `current`. `__init__` connects
  `self.currentRowChanged` to a new `_show_current(self, row: int)` that
  calls `set_current(i == row)` on every row in `self._rows`; `set_pages`
  (478-492) calls `_show_current(self.currentRow())` after rebuilding, because
  the rebuild happens with no row current and a blocker may be on
  (`workspace.py` 1208). The workspace's existing
  `page_strip.currentRowChanged.connect(self.show_page)` (1110) is untouched.
- Nothing else: no delegate change (`_NoTextDelegate`, 299-316, keeps
  painting the item ground under the row), no hover plumbing, no `running`.
