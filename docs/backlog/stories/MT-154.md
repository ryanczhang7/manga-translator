## Context

EPIC-13 priority 5. On p004 the user saw the balloon's English set at the
**top** of a large balloon instead of in its centre. `typeset/interior.py`
(MT-123) accepts the flood of light pixels as the balloon only if it lies
strictly inside a *window*: the region's bbox grown by `CAP_RATIO x max(w,
h)` = 1.0x per side. p004's balloon is 361 x 766 px. The region that carried
the sentence was one 98 x 185 px piece at its top, so the window reached only
to y ≈ 611 of a balloon ending at 991. The flood was judged a leak, and the
star fallback centred the text on the piece.

MT-144 makes that piece the whole balloon, which removes p004's case. But
the rule is wrong on its own terms: one short line in a big balloon, or a
small `はい` in a tall shout balloon, hits it too. The window was there to
stop a flood through an open outline into the page's white margin. The page
edge and the flood's size say that directly. A flood that touches no page
edge and covers at most `INTERIOR_MAX_PAGE_FRACTION` of the page is a closed
balloon, whatever its size relative to the text.

Required gates: `unit`, `coverage`.

## Acceptance criteria

On synthetic cleaned pages:

- **AC-1** - Given a closed 360 x 770 outline and a 98 x 185 polygon at its
  top, when `bubble_interior` runs, then it returns the balloon's interior,
  and its centroid is within 2 % of the balloon's.
- **AC-2** - Given the same polygon and outline, when `typeset` lays out a
  three-line string into the returned ring, then the block's vertical centre
  is within 5 % of the balloon height of the balloon's centre.
- **AC-3** - *(control: an open outline still falls back)* Given an outline
  with a gap opening onto the page's white margin, which touches a page edge,
  then the star is returned, as today.
- **AC-4** - *(control: a white panel is not a balloon)* Given a closed
  white area covering more than `INTERIOR_MAX_PAGE_FRACTION` of the page,
  then the star is returned.
- **AC-5** - Every existing case in `tests/core/test_typeset_interior.py` is
  unchanged, and RED records that in the handoff.

## Contract

**Writes:** `src/mangatl/typeset/interior.py`, `tests/core/test_typeset_interior.py`

- Rule 5 of the module docstring becomes: "a component that touches no page
  edge and whose area is at most `INTERIOR_MAX_PAGE_FRACTION` x the page is
  the interior". The window stays as the **star's** cap only, and
  `CAP_RATIO` keeps that role.
- `INTERIOR_MAX_PAGE_FRACTION: float` is new and **oracle-free**. GREEN
  derives it from MT-143's largest confirmed balloon against its largest
  white non-balloon area, and records the band. If MT-144's
  `BALLOON_MAX_PAGE_FRACTION` has landed by then, GREEN reads that measured
  band too. They answer the same question on two different images, and
  `typeset` may not import `detect`, so the value is repeated, with each
  constant's comment citing the other.
- `bubble_interior`'s signature is unchanged.
- Oracle partition: AC-1 to AC-5 are **mechanical**.
