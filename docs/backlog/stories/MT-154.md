## Context

EPIC-13 priority 5. On p004 the user saw the balloon's English set at the
**top** of a large balloon instead of in its centre. `typeset/interior.py`
(MT-123) accepts the flood of light pixels as the balloon only if it lies
strictly inside a *window*: the region's bbox grown by `CAP_RATIO x max(w,
h)` = 1.0x per side. The region that carried p004's sentence was one
98 x 185 px piece at the top of its balloon, so the window ended far above
the balloon's bottom. The flood was judged a leak, and the star fallback
centred the text on the piece.

MT-145 (which replaced MT-144) merges most such pieces, but the rule is wrong
on its own terms: one short line in a big balloon, or a small `はい` in a tall
shout balloon, hits it too.

**Re-planned 2026-11-19, before RED, on a measurement** (`## Notes`, N-1). The
story as cut replaced the window with "touches no page edge and covers at
most a fraction of the page". Measured on the development set's cleaned
pages, that rule changes 30 regions, and for 22 of them the flood is **not**
the balloon: it is the balloon plus the light art of its panel (a face, a
coat, paper-white background), reached through an open outline, and it
touches no page edge. Their text would move onto the art. No page fraction
separates them: the leaks span 0.018-0.092 of the page and real balloons
0.001-0.089. So the window was doing real work, and it stays.

What separates the two on that set is **shape**. A closed balloon is nearly
convex: its flood fills its own convex hull. A flood that ran out into a
panel does not. So this story *adds* a second way to be accepted, and removes
nothing: a flood that fails the window is still the interior when it touches
no page edge, covers at most `INTERIOR_MAX_PAGE_FRACTION` of the page, **and**
fills at least `INTERIOR_MIN_SOLIDITY` of its convex hull.

Gates that guard it: `unit` and `coverage-core` (both `required`;
`coverage-core` holds `src/mangatl/typeset/**` at 100 %).

## Acceptance criteria

On synthetic cleaned pages of 1414 x 2000 px (the development scans' size),
non-light (grey < 200) everywhere except where a criterion says otherwise.
"The window" is the polygon's bbox grown by 1.0 x max(w, h) per side, clipped
to the page, as MT-123 defined it.

- **AC-1** - Given a closed elliptical outline 360 x 770 px with a white
  interior, and a 98 x 185 `bubble` polygon at its top whose window ends above
  the balloon's bottom, when `bubble_interior` runs, then the returned ring's
  fill covers at least 95 % of the balloon's white interior, holds no outline
  pixel, and its centroid is within 2 % of the balloon's width horizontally
  and 2 % of its height vertically of the interior's centroid.
  *Control:* the rule as it is today returns the star around the piece, whose
  centroid sits about 30 % of the balloon's height above the balloon's centre.
- **AC-2** - Given the same page and polygon, when `typeset` lays out a string
  that breaks into three lines in the returned ring, then the block's vertical
  centre (midway between the first line's top and the last line's bottom) is
  within 5 % of the balloon's height of the balloon's centre.
  *Control:* today's ring puts it about 38 % of the balloon's height too high.
- **AC-3** - *(control: a balloon open onto the page edge still falls back)*
  Given the AC-1 balloon placed so that the page's right edge cuts it - its
  white reaches the edge, nothing else changes, and the flood is still convex
  and under the page-fraction limit - then the returned ring has no vertex
  outside the window.
- **AC-4** - *(control: a white panel is not a balloon)* Given a closed white
  rectangle covering more than `INTERIOR_MAX_PAGE_FRACTION` of the page,
  touching no page edge, with the AC-1 polygon inside it, then the returned
  ring has no vertex outside the window.
- **AC-5** - *(control: a balloon open into light art is not found whole)*
  Given the AC-1 balloon with a gap in its outline that opens into a white
  area of the panel beside it, where the whole flood touches no page edge,
  stays under the page-fraction limit and fills at most 85 % of its convex
  hull, then the returned ring has no vertex outside the window.
- **AC-6** - Every existing case in `tests/core/test_typeset_interior.py`
  passes with its assertions unedited, and RED records that in the handoff.

## Contract

**Writes:** `src/mangatl/typeset/interior.py`, `tests/core/test_typeset_interior.py`, `tests/core/_bubble_world.py`

RED may amend any block below, in place, with a reason; GREEN builds what the
amended block says. The acceptance criteria do not share that freedom.

**C-1 The rule.** `bubble_interior`'s rules 1-4 and 6-7 are unchanged. Rule 5
gains a second clause, tried only when the first fails:

- 5a (today's, unchanged): a component strictly inside the window is the
  interior.
- 5b (new): otherwise, a component is the interior when **all three** hold:
  1. it has no pixel in row 0, row H-1, column 0 or column W-1;
  2. `area <= INTERIOR_MAX_PAGE_FRACTION * H * W`, where `area` is the
     component's pixel count (before the union with the polygon fill), and
     the comparison is inclusive;
  3. `area / hull >= INTERIOR_MIN_SOLIDITY`, inclusive, where `hull` is
     `cv2.contourArea(cv2.convexHull(c))` for `c` the component's external
     contour (`cv2.findContours(..., RETR_EXTERNAL, CHAIN_APPROX_NONE)`; the
     component is connected, so there is one), floored at 1.0. A hull through
     pixel centres is slightly smaller than the pixels it holds, so this
     ratio can exceed 1 (1.002 measured on a rectangle). That is expected.
- Either way the interior is `component | fill`, as rule 5 says today.
- Anything else goes to rule 6, the star, with `CAP_RATIO` as its cap. The
  star is unchanged.

Because 5a is kept, **no region accepted today changes**. The module
docstring's rule list is rewritten to say 5a / 5b.

**C-2 The numbers. Both are settled: read them out, do not derive or tune.**

- `INTERIOR_MIN_SOLIDITY: float = 0.96`. Measured in planning on the
  development set's cleaned pages (N-1): the 6 closed balloons the window
  rejects score 0.977-1.002, and the 22 leaks score 0.596-0.941. 0.96 sits in
  that gap, nearer the positives because a leak accepted is text on a face
  and a balloon refused is only today's behaviour. Balloons with spiked or
  cloud outlines score lower (0.83 seen) and keep the star: a documented
  limit, not a defect of this story.
- `INTERIOR_MAX_PAGE_FRACTION: float = 0.25`. **A design bound, not a
  measured band** (PO-2): the development set has no closed flood above 0.092
  of the page, so nothing on it bounds this from above. It exists for AC-4's
  case, a plain white panel, which is convex and would pass clause 3.
- Both are exported in `__all__`. `CAP_RATIO`, `LIGHT_GREY`, `DARK_GREY` and
  `RAY_COUNT` keep their values and roles.

**C-3 Signature.** `bubble_interior(cleaned, polygon, kind) -> Ring` is
unchanged, so there is no caller list to check. For orientation only, its
callers are `src/mangatl/pipeline/bake.py` (`_set_blocks`),
`tests/core/test_typeset_interior.py`, `tests/core/test_bake_interior.py` and
