## Context

EPIC-13, after its closing audit. `docs/wiki/audits/MT-159-epic-13-done-when.md` (`## Evidence` E-6, "An aside on
the same balloon") found a thin vertical stroke left on p082's cleaned page,
inside one balloon, between regions r1 and r2. Those two regions are the two
pieces of one truth balloon, p082 t1. The OCR stored `君だー` for r1 and `ー`
for r2, and the truth document's note on t1 reads "long dash continues
below". So the stroke is a long dash, and each piece's mask covers one end
of it. MT-159's diff review, round 1, measured it read-only on the stored
pages: the source component is `293 710 3x173`, with 64 px inside r1's
stored mask and 103 px inside r2's; on the cleaned page the component is
`293 696 3x188`, 15 px longer than the source, with 67 px inside r1's mask
and 92 px inside r2's. So the ink left on the cleaned page lies inside both
masks as well as between them: a pixel being in a mask does not yet mean its
ink is gone. The audit's p075 t2 row (E-5, strokes drawn by the inpainter
inside an erased balloon) is a second case.

**The bench cannot see it.** p082 t1 is a missed truth balloon, and residual
ink is measured only on matched ones. So no "Done when" line moves with this
story. It is a defect a reader sees on the baked page: a line of Japanese
ink beside the English.

**The mechanism, and it was foreseen.** `clean/mask.py` grows the erase
mask over every ink component it touches, when the component is glyph-sized
and stands on paper (MT-152). `_glyphs_touched` skips a component wider or
taller than `GLYPH_MAX_SIDE_PX` (71), because by size alone a long mark
cannot be told from a balloon's outline. MT-152 measured this very mark at
planning, `p082 293 710 3x173` (3 px wide, 173 px long, 0.994 of its ring is
paper), left it "not judged", and put "marks longer than
`GLYPH_MAX_SIDE_PX`, such as p082's 173 px line" out of scope. Its GREEN
notes say "The 173 px line on p082 stays, as expected".

**The Lead PO's cut, open to the user.** One more clause in the mask's
growth: an ink component that is long, thin and on paper is added whole when
region masks cover **both** of its ends. Thinness is what size could not
say: an outline is long on both sides, and a dash is long on one. Both ends
is what keeps a frame line, a speed line or a balloon's tail out, which a
single glyph can touch at one end. MT-152's standing rule is kept: where no
setting grows the dash and no outline, the outline and the art win.

**To measure before the Contract is written.** On the no-spend project
`D:\bench\blackjack-v1-ep02-03-mt159.mtproj` and the development pages
(read-only, no pipeline run needed):

- that the stroke left on p082 is the component MT-152 measured, and that
  r1's and r2's masks each touch it, at opposite ends (the diff review's
  figures above say so; re-read them, do not re-derive);
- how much of the cleaned page's stroke lies inside the masks, and why: by
  pixel and by row, against each region's stored mask and against the mask
  cleaning used (`erase_mask(regions, size, page)`, grown and dilated), and
  how far the cleaned component runs past the source component's ends. If
  ink is left inside the mask cleaning used, the planner says before the
  Contract is written whether covering the whole stroke removes it, or
  whether the inpainter redraws a stroke it was asked to erase, which this
  story's clause does not fix. AC-9 depends on the answer;
- on all 46 pages, every ink component longer than `GLYPH_MAX_SIDE_PX` that
  the union of masks touches, with its short side, its ring fraction and
  whether masks cover one end or both; each one a both-ends rule would grow
  is looked at as a crop.

If a both-ends rule would grow anything that is not a dash, the planner
reports it to the user before any criterion is relied on.

**What is settled.** MT-152's constants and its rule that outlines and art
win. That the erase mask is the only place cleaning decides what to touch,
and that baking restores an excluded region through the same mask.

**What is evidence, to verify.** That the stroke is one connected component,
is MT-152's `3x173`, and has masks at both of its ends: measured once, by
the diff review, on the stored masks. That ink inside a mask goes once the
whole stroke is masked: not shown. Today 67 px and 92 px of the cleaned
stroke lie inside r1's and r2's masks.

**Required gate that would fail if this broke:** `unit`.

## Acceptance criteria

On synthetic pages, as MT-152's criteria are: white paper, black strokes, a
dark balloon outline, a screentone patch. "The mask" of a region is its own
stored mask, before any dilation. `DASH_MAX_WIDTH_PX` stands for the limit
on a dash's short side; the Contract names it and GREEN derives its value on
the development set, never on pages 3 to 58.

- **AC-1** - Given a straight vertical stroke on white paper, 3 px wide and
  173 px long, and two regions whose masks cover its top 20 px and its
  bottom 20 px, when `erase_mask(regions, size, page)` runs, then the result
  is exactly the two masks united with the whole stroke, dilated by
  `MASK_DILATION_PX`: every pixel of the stroke is in it, and nothing else
  is added.
- **AC-2** - *(control: one end)* Given the same stroke and only the region
  covering its top, when `erase_mask(regions, size, page)` runs, then the
  result equals `erase_mask(regions, size)`: no pixel of the stroke is
  added. A long thin mark touched at one end is left as today.
- **AC-3** - *(control: the outline)* Given a balloon outline, one
  component longer than `GLYPH_MAX_SIDE_PX` on both sides, and two regions
  whose masks touch it at two places, when `erase_mask(regions, size, page)`
  runs, then the result equals `erase_mask(regions, size)`: no outline pixel
  is added.
  *Control:* the same page and masks with `DASH_MAX_WIDTH_PX` raised above
  the outline's shorter side **does** add the outline, so the width rule is
  what held it back.
- **AC-4** - *(control: art)* Given AC-1's stroke and masks with the stroke
  inside a screentone patch, so that under `GLYPH_PAPER_RING_FRACTION` of
  its ring is paper, then the result equals `erase_mask(regions, size)`.
  *Control:* the same stroke and masks on white paper is AC-1, and is added
  whole.
- **AC-5** - Given AC-1's stroke turned to run horizontally, with masks
  covering its left and right ends, then it is added whole: the short side
  is whichever side is shorter.
- **AC-6** - With `page=None`, or with no `page` argument, the mask is
  exactly today's. On every page of MT-152's criteria the mask is exactly
  what it is today: its tests pass unchanged, and no component at or under
  `GLYPH_MAX_SIDE_PX` is treated differently.
- **AC-7** - Given AC-1's page cleaned through `clean_page_image`, when the
  chapter is baked with both regions **included**, then on the stroke's
  pixels the baked page equals the cleaned page, not the source. When it is
  baked with both regions **excluded**, then on every pixel of the grown
  mask the baked page equals the source, the whole stroke included.
- **AC-8** - Given the development set's 46 pages, when the erase mask is
  built with the new clause, then the story records every component the
  clause adds, by page, position and size, each looked at as a crop. p082's
  stroke is among them, and no balloon outline, frame line or art is.
  *Control:* each negative MT-152 named (`p075 2 258 168x161`, `p075 418
  1115 93x91`, `p066 548 97 87x103`, `p075 529 66 45x104`, `p094 304 250
  102x191`) gains 0 px. And with the both-ends condition removed, the list
  is longer or the same, and the story says by which components: that is
  what the condition costs or saves.
- **AC-9** - Given p082 of the development set, its stored regions and its
  source scan, when the page is cleaned through `clean_page_image` with the
  new clause in, then the cleaned page holds no ink component (grey at or
  under `GLYPH_INK_GREY`, 8-connected) that is at most 8 px wide, over
  `GLYPH_MAX_SIDE_PX` tall and overlaps the box x 290 to 298, y 690 to 890.
  Today it holds one, `293 696 3x188`. Masked is not the claim; gone is.
  *Control:* with the clause removed, the same check finds that component,
  so the check can fail.
