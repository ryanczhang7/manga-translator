## Context

EPIC-13 priority 1, the second half of PO-1. MT-144 merges the pieces of a
**closed balloon**. Text with no closed balloon has nothing to merge by
except its own geometry: narration over art, a caption, a box (p058
`医者って一体` in five pieces, p011's caption `て――` in two). Today
`merge_columns` joins only **adjacent** columns. Its overlap clause asks
that two regions share at least half of the shorter one's extent *along* the
run direction, and two pieces stacked in one column share none of it. On the
benchmark, 28 in-balloon piece pairs that share a column sit 0-10 px apart
along it (EPIC-13 "Evidence"), and the rule can never join them.

This story adds the stacked case to the same rule, in the same fence box:
two regions are one utterance when they share at least
`COLUMN_MIN_OVERLAP` of the narrower one's extent **across** the run, and the
gap **along** the run is at most `COLUMN_MAX_RUN_GAP_PX`. MT-008's two
counter-examples must still hold.

**Widened at PLANNED, 2026-11-11 - MT-144 folded in (the user's decision).**
MT-144's rule (merge the pieces that share one closed balloon's paper) was
dropped in its GREEN: on the MT-143 development set, joined balloons share
wide paper with their neighbours (p063's narration box flush against a
balloon, p089's two overlapping balloons), and the user decided such balloons
**stay separate**; no setting of that rule kept them apart (MT-144 `## Notes`).
The gaps between pieces do separate them, so this story now owns **both**
directions:

- **Side by side** (adjacent columns of one balloon): MT-143 measured 12-25 px
  across (one at 38, p104), against different balloons in one fence box at
  37 px or more (the one exception, p075's touching pair, is caught by the
  overlap clause instead). Today's `COLUMN_MAX_GAP_PX = 12` was measured on
  1125 px scans and splits these 1414 px pages' columns; it is **re-derived
  here** on the development set.
- **Stacked** (pieces along one column): as above.

The fence-box clause and `COLUMN_MIN_OVERLAP` are unchanged.

Required gates: `unit`, `coverage`.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** - Given two regions in one fence box, one column wide, the second
  starting 6 px below the first ends (`axis="vertical"`), when
  `merge_columns` runs, then they come back as one region with
  `merged_from == (0, 1)`.
- **AC-2** - Given five stacked pieces of one column in one fence box, with
  gaps at or under `COLUMN_MAX_RUN_GAP_PX`, when merged, then one region
  comes back. The answer is the same for every input order of the five (the
  relation is closed transitively, as today).
- **AC-3** - *(control: the run gap)* Given two pieces stacked in one fence
  box with a gap of `COLUMN_MAX_RUN_GAP_PX + 1`, when merged, then two
  regions come back.
- **AC-4** - *(control: across-run overlap)* Given two stacked pieces in one
  fence whose horizontal extents share less than `COLUMN_MIN_OVERLAP` of the
  narrower one (a staggered layout), when merged, then two regions come back.
- **AC-5** - *(control: different fences)* Given two stacked pieces 2 px
  apart in **different** fence boxes, when merged, then two regions come
  back.
- **AC-6** - MT-008's corpus cases still pass unchanged. RED reruns
  `tests/core/test_detect_columns.py` as it stands, and records in the handoff
  that it is green on the new rule.
- **AC-7** - With `axis="horizontal"`, AC-1's geometry transposed merges the
  same way. The rule is written once, for vertical text, through
  `_oriented`.
- **AC-8** - Given two adjacent columns in one fence box, fully overlapping
  along the run, with a gap across the run of exactly `COLUMN_MAX_GAP_PX`,
  when merged, then one region comes back; with `COLUMN_MAX_GAP_PX + 1`, two.
  (Written relative to the constant, never to a literal.)
- **AC-9** - *(control: joined balloons stay separate)* Given two regions in
  one fence box that **touch** (gap 0 both ways) but share less than
  `COLUMN_MIN_OVERLAP` of the narrower one's extent in either direction - the
  p075 shape - when merged, then two regions come back.
- **AC-10** - *(control: the chain)* `tests/core/test_detect_page.py`'s
  existing chain tests still pass unchanged, including
  `test_two_separate_bubbles_in_one_box_are_not_merged_by_the_chain` (two
  bubbles 42 px apart in one box).

## Contract

**Writes:** `src/mangatl/detect/columns.py`, `tests/core/test_detect_columns.py`

```python
COLUMN_MAX_RUN_GAP_PX: int   # new; the widest empty gap ALONG the run that is
                             # still one column of one utterance, page pixels
def _is_one_utterance(a: Rect, b: Rect) -> bool:
    # unchanged clause OR the new stacked clause:
    #   along-run gap = max(0, max(a[1], b[1]) - min(a[3], b[3])) <= COLUMN_MAX_RUN_GAP_PX
    #   across-run shared = max(0, min(a[2], b[2]) - max(a[0], b[0]))
    #   shared / min(a[2] - a[0], b[2] - b[0]) >= COLUMN_MIN_OVERLAP
```

- `COLUMN_MAX_RUN_GAP_PX` is derived in GREEN from MT-143's measured gaps:
  pieces of one balloon along one column, against **different** utterances
  stacked in one fence box. The comment cites the band and the separation, as
  `COLUMN_MAX_GAP_PX`'s does. It is **never** read off the benchmark, whose
  0-10 px figure is diagnosis only.
- The constant is a pixel count, like `COLUMN_MAX_GAP_PX`, and has the same
  resolution limit. It is recorded in the comment, not solved here.
- Oracle partition: AC-1 to AC-7 are **mechanical**. The constant is
  **oracle-free**, and AC-3 is written relative to it (`+ 1`), never to a
  literal.
- Callers: `merge_columns`' signature is unchanged. `_is_one_utterance` is
  private.

**Added at PLANNED, 2026-11-11 (MT-144 folded in; lead-po, orchestrator).
RED may amend any block in place with a dated reason; GREEN builds what the
amended block says.**

- **`COLUMN_MAX_GAP_PX` is re-derived in GREEN on the MT-143 development
  set**, not left at 12: the across-run gaps between adjacent columns of one
  balloon (MT-143 audit item 3: 12, 13, 14, 14, 20, 25, 38) against different
  balloons in one fence box (37-71, median 53; p075's touching pair excluded,
  which the overlap clause separates). Its comment cites that band, the value
  chosen and the separation, replacing the 1125 px note. **Bounds the tree
  already fixes:** `tests/core/test_detect_columns.py:476` pins
  `8 <= COLUMN_MAX_GAP_PX <= 53` (MT-008's corpus: below 8 two merges are lost,
  at 54 a separate bubble is absorbed); `tests/core/test_detect_page.py`'s
  chain test keeps two bubbles **42 px** apart separate; the development set's
  nearest separate pair across is **37 px**. So the value lies in **[25, 36]**:
  at least the 25 px that covers every development pair but p104's 38, at most
  36. A development measurement that wants anything outside that band comes
  back to the user. p104's 38 px pair is then expected to stay split, and the
  audit says so.
- `COLUMN_MAX_RUN_GAP_PX` likewise: MT-143's adjacent along-run gaps (0-17 px
  for 57 of 65; tail 24-29, p082 116, p105 58) against the one
  different-utterance along pair in a fence (p075 2/3, gap 0, column overlap
  0.156 - separated by the overlap clause, not the gap).
- **No balloon or paper test.** The rule decides on the regions' extents, the
  fence box and the two overlaps only; joined balloons (p063, p089, p095,
  p098, p104 per MT-144's sweep) must come back separate.
- Oracle partition: AC-8 to AC-10 are **mechanical**; AC-8 is written relative
  to the constant.
- Callers of the changed **value**: `rg COLUMN_MAX_GAP_PX` (2026-11-11) - only
  `src/mangatl/detect/columns.py` and `tests/core/test_detect_columns.py`
  (lines 27, 59, 245-251, 330, 476, 570-578, 644), every use written relative
  to the constant except the band pin at 476, which the new value satisfies.
  `tests/core/test_detect_page.py`'s chain tests are behaviour callers
  (AC-10). RED states in the handoff that this list was checked against the
  tree.
