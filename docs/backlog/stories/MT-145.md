## Context

EPIC-13 priority 1, the second half of PO-1. MT-144 merges the pieces of a
**closed balloon**. Text with no closed balloon has nothing to merge by
except its own geometry: narration over art, a caption, a box (p058
`医者って一体` in five pieces, p011's caption `て――` in two). Today
`merge_columns` joins only **adjacent** columns. Its overlap clause asks
that two regions share at least half of the shorter one's extent *along* the
run direction, and two pieces stacked in one column share none of it. On the
benchmark, 28 in-balloon piece pairs that share a column sit 0-10 px apart
along it (EPIC-13 "Evidence"), and the rule can never join them.

This story adds the stacked case to the same rule, in the same fence box:
two regions are one utterance when they share at least
`COLUMN_MIN_OVERLAP` of the narrower one's extent **across** the run, and the
gap **along** the run is at most `COLUMN_MAX_RUN_GAP_PX`. The existing
clauses are untouched, and MT-008's two counter-examples must still hold.

Required gates: `unit`, `coverage`.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** - Given two regions in one fence box, one column wide, the second
  starting 6 px below the first ends (`axis="vertical"`), when
  `merge_columns` runs, then they come back as one region with
  `merged_from == (0, 1)`.
- **AC-2** - Given five stacked pieces of one column in one fence box, with
  gaps at or under `COLUMN_MAX_RUN_GAP_PX`, when merged, then one region
  comes back. The answer is the same for every input order of the five (the
  relation is closed transitively, as today).
- **AC-3** - *(control: the run gap)* Given two pieces stacked in one fence
  box with a gap of `COLUMN_MAX_RUN_GAP_PX + 1`, when merged, then two
  regions come back.
- **AC-4** - *(control: across-run overlap)* Given two stacked pieces in one
  fence whose horizontal extents share less than `COLUMN_MIN_OVERLAP` of the
  narrower one (a staggered layout), when merged, then two regions come back.
- **AC-5** - *(control: different fences)* Given two stacked pieces 2 px
  apart in **different** fence boxes, when merged, then two regions come
  back.
- **AC-6** - MT-008's corpus cases still pass unchanged. RED reruns
  `tests/core/test_detect_columns.py` as it stands, and records in the handoff
  that it is green on the new rule.
- **AC-7** - With `axis="horizontal"`, AC-1's geometry transposed merges the
  same way. The rule is written once, for vertical text, through
  `_oriented`.

## Contract

**Writes:** `src/mangatl/detect/columns.py`, `tests/core/test_detect_columns.py`

```python
COLUMN_MAX_RUN_GAP_PX: int   # new; the widest empty gap ALONG the run that is
                             # still one column of one utterance, page pixels
def _is_one_utterance(a: Rect, b: Rect) -> bool:
    # unchanged clause OR the new stacked clause:
    #   along-run gap = max(0, max(a[1], b[1]) - min(a[3], b[3])) <= COLUMN_MAX_RUN_GAP_PX
    #   across-run shared = max(0, min(a[2], b[2]) - max(a[0], b[0]))
    #   shared / min(a[2] - a[0], b[2] - b[0]) >= COLUMN_MIN_OVERLAP
```

- `COLUMN_MAX_RUN_GAP_PX` is derived in GREEN from MT-143's measured gaps:
  pieces of one balloon along one column, against **different** utterances
  stacked in one fence box. The comment cites the band and the separation, as
  `COLUMN_MAX_GAP_PX`'s does. It is **never** read off the benchmark, whose
  0-10 px figure is diagnosis only.
- The constant is a pixel count, like `COLUMN_MAX_GAP_PX`, and has the same
  resolution limit. It is recorded in the comment, not solved here.
- Oracle partition: AC-1 to AC-7 are **mechanical**. The constant is
  **oracle-free**, and AC-3 is written relative to it (`+ 1`), never to a
  literal.
- Callers: `merge_columns`' signature is unchanged. `_is_one_utterance` is
  private.
