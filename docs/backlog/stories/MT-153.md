## Context

EPIC-13 priority 4. In p004's large white balloon the user saw faint ghosts
of the erased Japanese. Looked at in the cleaned page, they are patches a
few grey levels off the surrounding paper, in the shape of the erase mask.
LaMa reconstructs flat white as near-white, and the eye picks out the edge
where the fill meets the untouched paper. S2's residual metric cannot see it:
it counts pixels darker than 160 with local contrast of at least 64, and
these differ by single-digit levels. So this story brings its own
measurement.

When every pixel in a component's surrounding ring is the same paper (low
spread), there is nothing for the model to reconstruct. The right fill is
that paper. So a component on flat paper is filled with the ring's median
colour, without calling the model. Anything with texture (screentone, art,
gradients) still goes to LaMa.

**Measured at PLANNED on the development set** (Contract C-6, never the
benchmark pages): the erase mask has 346 components on 46 pages. Where the
ring is flat, LaMa's fill is off the paper by 1 to 2 levels at its worst pixel
on most components, and by about 200 on three where it painted the `……` dots
back (p084). So the fill removes a faint tint on most balloons and real ink on
a few.

Required gates: `unit`, `coverage`, and `integration` for this story
(`required_gates`; `## Notes` PO-2 says why).

## Acceptance criteria

"Ring", "spread", "median" and "flat" are Contract C-2's. `ghost` is AC-5's.

- **AC-1** - Given a hole holding black ink on paper of constant value 252
  (with a ring of `FLAT_RING_PX` around it all 252), when `clean_page` runs,
  then every hole pixel is exactly 252, and the session is **not called** for
  that component. This holds whatever path the hole's size would have taken:
  native, strips or downscale.
- **AC-2** - Given paper with JPEG-like noise of ±2 levels round 252 (every
  ring value in 250..254, both extremes present), then the ring's
  spread is at or under `FLAT_MAX_SPREAD`, the session is not called, and the
  fill is the ring's median. The maximum absolute difference between the
  filled hole and the ring median is 0.
- **AC-3** - *(control: texture)* Given a hole on a screentone dot pattern,
  then the ring's spread exceeds `FLAT_MAX_SPREAD` and the session is called,
  as today: the hole holds the session's output.
- **AC-4** - *(control: a ring crossing an outline)* Given AC-1's page with
  outline ink in the hole's ring, then the session is called. Spread is
  measured over the whole ring, and a single dark pixel in it defeats "flat".
  *Control:* the same dark pixel moved just beyond the ring leaves the
  component flat, and it is filled as in AC-1.
- **AC-5** - *(the ghost, measured)* Define `ghost(page, mask)` as the
  maximum, over the hole's pixels and the three channels, of the absolute
  difference from the ring median. Given a session double that returns paper
  minus 6 levels (246 on the 252 paper; a faint tint of the kind LaMa leaves,
  stood in by a double), then the cleaned AC-1 page scores 0. *Controls, that
  the metric can see a ghost:* the same page with the flat fill switched off
  (C-2) scores 6, and AC-4's page, which is not flat, scores 6 with the fill
  on.
- **AC-6** - *(one page, both kinds)* Given a page with one hole on flat paper
  and one on screentone, then the session is called for the screentone hole
  only, its mask holds none of the flat hole's pixels, the flat hole is
  filled as in AC-1, and no pixel outside the erase mask changes.

### C-2 The flat test and the fill

For each 8-connected component of `mask`, in ascending label order, **before
the path is chosen**:

1. **Ring.** The component dilated by the elliptical kernel of side
   `2 * FLAT_RING_PX + 1` (`cv2.MORPH_ELLIPSE`, the element
   `clean/mask.py`'s `_dilated` uses), **minus every pixel of the whole erase
   mask**, clipped to the page. Off-page pixels are not counted.
   - Minus the whole mask, not only this component: a neighbouring
     component's ink is still on the page when this one is judged, and it is
     about to be erased. Counting it would send every glyph beside another
     glyph to the model.
   - So the ring holds only pixels `clean_page` never writes. The verdict is
     the same read from `page` or from the working result, and does not depend
     on the order of the components.
2. **An empty ring is not flat.** The component takes the model, as today.
   `FLAT_RING_PX = 0` therefore switches the fill off.
3. **Spread** is the largest, over the three channels, of that channel's
   maximum minus its minimum over the ring. No grey conversion.
   - This amends the 2026-11-07 draft's "luma". A luma difference is a
     weighted mean of the channel differences, so it never exceeds the largest
     of them: per channel calls nothing flat that luma would not. It also sees
     a colour cast of constant luma, and the ghost on p089 is a cast.
4. **Flat** means `spread <= FLAT_MAX_SPREAD`. Inclusive: a ring whose spread
   equals the limit is filled. A negative limit therefore switches the fill
   off, and **`FLAT_MAX_SPREAD = -1` is the off switch tests use** (C-5).
5. **Median** is per channel, the *lower* median: the value at index
   `(n - 1) // 2` of that channel's `n` ring values sorted ascending. It is
   always a value some ring pixel has in that channel, and never a `.5`.
6. **Fill.** A flat component has the median written to exactly its own
   pixels in the working result. The session is not called for it, on any
   path: the check comes before the native / strips / downscale choice
   (MT-151's strips included).
7. **Not flat:** the component takes the path it takes today, unchanged.

What follows from 6: a later component's window is cut from the working
result, so the model sees an earlier flat component as filled paper, where
today it sees LaMa's fill of it. Label order is unchanged, and a later
component's ink is visible context exactly as today.

Not built here: filling every flat component in a first pass so the model
never sees their ink. It would change what the model is given for every
textured component at once, and nothing measured asks for it.

### C-3 The semantics behind the numbers

| Number | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `FLAT_RING_PX` | Ring radius. A pixel is in the ring when the elliptical element centred on it reaches the component, so "all 252 within `FLAT_RING_PX`" (AC-1) and "just beyond the ring" (AC-4) are read off that element, not off a Euclidean distance. |
| `FLAT_MAX_SPREAD` | Compared with `<=`. In 8-bit levels of one channel. AC-2 puts it at 4 or more; the development set's tone puts a ceiling on it (C-6). |
| 252 | The fixture's paper. Not 255, so that a fill that writes white instead of the ring's own colour fails AC-1. |
| 6 | AC-5's double is 6 levels under the paper. A stand-in size, far below S2's contrast of 64. The planning probe measured LaMa's own worst-pixel difference at 1 to 2 levels on most flat components (C-6). |

### For the implementation

- The prototype that passed everything is about 30 lines: per component, crop
  the bbox plus `FLAT_RING_PX`, dilate the component with
  `cv2.MORPH_ELLIPSE`, subtract `mask` (the whole erase mask, cropped), read
