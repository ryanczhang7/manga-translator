## Context

EPIC-13 priority 4. In p004's large white balloon the user saw faint ghosts
of the erased Japanese. Looked at in the cleaned page, they are rectangles a
few grey levels off the surrounding paper, in the shape of the glyph masks.
LaMa reconstructs flat white as near-white, and the eye picks out the edge
where the fill meets the untouched paper. S2's residual metric cannot see it:
it counts pixels darker than 160 with local contrast of at least 64, and
these differ by single-digit levels. So this story brings its own
measurement.

When every pixel in a component's surrounding ring is the same paper (low
spread), there is nothing for the model to reconstruct. The right fill is
that paper. So a component on flat paper is filled with the ring's median
colour, without calling the model. Anything with texture (screentone, art,
gradients) still goes to LaMa.

Required gates: `unit`, `coverage`.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** - Given a hole on paper of constant value 252 (with a ring of
  `FLAT_RING_PX` around it all 252), when `clean_page` runs, then every hole
  pixel is exactly 252, and the session is **not called** for that component.
- **AC-2** - Given paper with JPEG-like noise of ±2 levels, then the ring's
  spread is at or under `FLAT_MAX_SPREAD` and the fill is the ring's median.
  The maximum absolute difference between the filled hole and the ring median
  is 0.
- **AC-3** - *(control: texture)* Given a hole on a screentone dot pattern,
  then the ring's spread exceeds `FLAT_MAX_SPREAD` and the session is called,
  as today.
- **AC-4** - *(control: a ring crossing an outline)* Given a hole whose ring
  includes outline ink, then the session is called. Spread is measured over
  the whole ring, and a dark pixel in it defeats "flat".
- **AC-5** - *(the ghost, measured)* Define `ghost(page, mask)` as the
  maximum, over the hole, of the absolute difference from the ring median.
  Given a session double that returns paper minus 6 levels (LaMa's measured
  behaviour stood in by a double), then the fill scores 0 on AC-1's page. The
  double alone, on a textured page, scores 6. This is the control that the
  metric can see a ghost.

## Contract

**Writes:** `src/mangatl/clean/inpaint.py`, `tests/core/test_clean_inpaint.py`

- `FLAT_RING_PX: int` and `FLAT_MAX_SPREAD: int` are new. Spread is max minus
  min of luma over the component's dilated ring, in the working result.
- The flat check runs per component before the path choice. A flat component
  takes no model call on any path, MT-151's strips included.
- `FLAT_MAX_SPREAD` is **oracle-free**, and AC-2 and AC-3 bracket it. GREEN
  measures paper spread and tone spread on MT-143's pages and records the
  band.
- MT-151 writes the same file. Different branches of `clean_page`, so this
  is a different wave, not a dependency.
- Oracle partition: AC-1 to AC-4 are **mechanical**. AC-5 is
  **oracle-free**, and its control is stated in the criterion.
