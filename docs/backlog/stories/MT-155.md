## Context

EPIC-13 priority 5, and **conditional** (PO-6). On the benchmark, where one
balloon held two regions, each region was fitted into the same balloon
interior and set on top of the other. That is why "One point" sits over "is
a flat rate of 10 yen." on p023, and why the text is tiny on p011, p016, p017
and p018 (`pipeline/bake.py` `_set_blocks`: one `typeset` per region).
MT-144 and MT-145 remove most such pairs at detection.

**This story runs only if pairs remain.** At PLANNED → RED the PO re-counts,
on the MT-143 development set detected with MT-144 and MT-145 merged, the
included regions whose `bubble_interior` rings are the same balloon (IoU at
least 0.9). If the count is **zero**, the story closes as a no-op with the
count cited, as MT-121 did. Otherwise it goes ahead. A pair can survive
detection: a balloon whose outline the source-page flood sees as open, where
the cleaned page's does not.

Required gates: `unit`, `coverage`.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** - Given two included regions on one page whose interiors are the
  same balloon, with proposals "One point" (reading index 8) and "is a flat
  rate of 10 yen." (index 9), when the page is baked, then **one** block is
  set in that interior. Its text is the two lines joined by one space in
  reading order, and no glyph of one is drawn over the other.
- **AC-2** - Given the same pair, the block's size is the size `typeset`
  chooses for the joined text in that interior, and it is at least the size
  chosen for the smaller of the two alone.
- **AC-3** - *(control: two balloons)* Given two regions in two different
  balloons with interior IoU under 0.9, then two blocks are set, as today.
- **AC-4** - An excluded region never joins a block, and a region with no
  text to set adds nothing to it.
- **AC-5** - The review screen and the store are unchanged: two lines stay
  two lines. Only the bake joins them.

## Contract

**Writes:** `src/mangatl/pipeline/bake.py`, `tests/core/test_bake_interior.py`

- `SHARED_INTERIOR_IOU: float = 0.9` is the Lead PO's choice, not tuned, and
  it is **settled** for this story: two rings of one flood differ only by the
  polygon fills united into them.
- `_set_blocks` groups the `(index, interior, text)` triples by shared
  interior, in reading order, before calling `typeset` once per group. The
  ring used is the union of the group's rings.
- MT-152 also writes `pipeline/bake.py`. Different functions, so this is a
  different wave.
- Oracle partition: AC-1 to AC-5 are **mechanical**.
