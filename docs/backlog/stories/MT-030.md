## Context

**Filed 2026-09-12 out of MT-002's first escalation, on the user's decision to
settle it in parallel rather than defer it to MT-007.**

MT-002 settled the *runtime* — ONNX Runtime on the CUDA execution provider, all
three models working on the RTX 5070. Read
`docs/wiki/audits/MT-002-model-runtime.md` `## Decided` and **do not
re-litigate the runtime**. This story is only about *which detector*, and its
`## Evidence` is re-verifiable as always.

What MT-002 found, and could not resolve:

- `dmMaze/comic-text-detector` is **GPL-3.0** [measured — the repo's `LICENSE`].
- The ONNX re-upload MT-002 actually measured,
  `mayocream/comic-text-detector-onnx`, declares **`apache-2.0`** [web] — a third
  party's metadata on a derivative of a GPL-3.0 project, which does not
  obviously relicense anything.
- Upstream states the model was trained on ~13k images, **1/3 from Manga109-s**
  [web], a dataset whose terms restrict use to academic research.

This application is **distributed** as a double-click installer (brief §2,
MT-024), which is what makes this live rather than academic.

**Why now rather than at MT-007.** If the answer is "find another detector",
that is research, and MT-007's `## Contract` model identity changes with it.
Four stories sit between here and MT-007, so this runs alongside them at no cost
to the critical path — and discovering it inside MT-007's RED would put research
on the critical path instead. MT-007 now `depends_on` this story.

**Which required gate would fail if this story's artifact broke:** none — the
artifact is a document. That is why this is a `spike` and not a `feature`, and
why its phase path is PLANNED → REVIEW → DONE.

## Acceptance criteria

A spike's criteria are about what is *known* at the end, not about behaviour.

- **AC-1** — `docs/wiki/audits/MT-030-detector-licence.md` exists and lists every
  candidate detector considered, and for each: its licence, whether a usable
  ONNX export exists and where, what its training data is stated to be, and
  whether that training data carries use restrictions. Candidates ruled out are
  listed with the reason, not silently dropped.
- **AC-2** — for each candidate that is both permissively licensed **and** has an
  ONNX export, the document records a measured forward pass on the **same
  fixture pages MT-002 used** (`spikes/MT-002/pages/011–015.jpg`), on this
  machine, reporting region count, where the regions fell, and the execution
  provider actually selected — `session.get_providers()`, never
  `get_available_providers()`; MT-002 E2 says why.
- **AC-3** — the document quotes, rather than paraphrases, (a) the incumbent's
  actual licence text where it bears on distributing a derived work, and (b) the
  Manga109-s terms as published. A summary of a licence is not a licence.
- **AC-4** — the document ends with a `## Decided` section naming the detector
  MT-007 should use and its file identity (URL + SHA-256), **or** stating that
  no permissively-licensed alternative was found and that the decision is
  therefore between shipping GPL-3.0 and commissioning a detector — with the
  evidence for that, and without pretending the choice is technical.
- **AC-5** — anything reported as a *measurement* is labelled with what it was
  measured on; anything reported as a *claim from the web* is labelled as such.
  Same rule as MT-002 AC-5, same reason.

## Contract

**Output paths:**

- `docs/wiki/audits/MT-030-detector-licence.md` — the decision document.
- `docs/wiki/architecture.md` — a new decision record **only if** the detector
  changes, or if "ship GPL knowingly" is chosen. A licence position that is not
  written into the architecture is not a position.
- `spikes/MT-030/` — throwaway probe code and any downloaded candidates.
  `.gitignore` covers `spikes/`.

**The `## Decided` / `## Evidence` split is load-bearing**, exactly as in MT-002.
`## Decided` carries the detector choice and file identity and nothing else;
every number lives in `## Evidence` and is re-verified by MT-007, not assumed.

**Baselines to read out of MT-002 rather than re-derive** — these are the bar a
replacement has to clear, and they are `## Evidence`, so **re-verify them before
comparing against them**:

- recall **0.949**, IoU **0.585** against the detector author's own reference
  mask (MT-002 E6). Precision is low there because MT-002's post-processing was
  unrefined, not because the model is.
- **9/9** correct end-to-end transcriptions on page `014.jpg` via detector
  boxes, including white-on-black text (MT-002 E5/E6).
- The box head and the segmentation mask **disagree on sound effects**: boxes
  skip the パチ, the mask covers them (MT-002). Any replacement must be checked
  for the same split, because MT-007 AC-6 and MT-019 both depend on it.

**Oracle partition: oracle-free**, as MT-002 was. There is no settled answer to
read out. So the demand falls on the negative side: a claim that a candidate is
"comparable" must state what would have counted as **worse**, before the
comparison — a region-count floor, a recall figure, a named failure on the same
fixture — or it is an impression.

## Out of scope

Anything about the runtime — settled by MT-002. Any production code. OCR and
inpainting model choice. Detection *thresholds*, which are MT-007's and are
test-first. Training or fine-tuning a detector: if the conclusion is that one
must be commissioned, that conclusion is the deliverable, not the model.

## Notes

**Timebox.** If no permissively-licensed detector with a usable ONNX export is
found within the spike, the honest outcome is a document that says so, lists
what was checked, and puts the two remaining options to the user with the
evidence. That is a success. Spending the spike trying to make a marginal
candidate work is not.

**This story does not give legal advice and must not pretend to.** Its job is to
put the actual licence text, the actual dataset terms and the actual technical
alternatives in front of the user in one place. Whether GPL-3.0 obligations are
acceptable for this product is the user's decision, and it may be one they want
counsel on. An agent's reading of a licence goes in the document labelled as
exactly that.
