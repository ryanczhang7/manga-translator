## Context

EPIC-13 priority 1 (U-1), and PO-1 decides the rule. On the benchmark,
**15 balloons came back as 53 regions**. 51 of the 82 spurious proposals are
those pieces, and 7 of the 20 missed balloons are ones no single piece
matches. Each piece is translated separately and set on top of the others
(p004, p016, p023, p053). Two pieces sit inside a whole region's outline
(p008).

`detect/columns.py` `merge_columns` merges only adjacent columns, and only
when they are at most 12 px apart in one fence box. Pieces of one balloon on
the 1414 px benchmark scans are 14-19 px apart across columns, and stacked
0-10 px apart along a column (EPIC-13 "Evidence"). Widening the gap walks
towards MT-008's measured negative, separate bubbles at gap 54 (PO-1). So
this story merges by the thing that actually separates balloons, the outline:
**regions whose text lies inside one closed balloon on the source page
become one region.**

`detect` may not import `typeset` (`architecture.md` §3), so this is a
flood of its own on the **source** page, with the regions' own pixels painted
out first. `typeset/interior.py` floods the *cleaned* page, which does not
exist yet at detect time. The two may share an idea, but they share no code.

Required gates: `unit`, `coverage`. The real-page check is a deferred
verification on the development set (DV-2), not an integration test: the
pages are outside the repo, and a skipped test in a binding gate verifies
nothing.

## Acceptance criteria

All on synthetic pages built in the test (white paper, a closed dark outline
of 3 px, black glyph blobs inside):

- **AC-1** - Given one closed balloon holding three regions (two adjacent
  columns 19 px apart, and a third stacked 6 px below one of them), when
  `merge_by_balloon` runs, then it returns **one** region. Its mask is the
  union of the three masks, its `merged_from` holds the three input
  positions, and it is placed in `(y0, x0)` order among the rest.
- **AC-2** - Given two regions inside a closed balloon and a third region
  lying wholly inside the second's polygon (the p008 shape), when merged, then
  one region comes back.
- **AC-3** - *(control: the MT-008 negative)* Given two separate closed
  balloons whose outlines are 3 px apart, each holding one region, when
  merged, then both come back unchanged, and `merged_from == ()` on each.
- **AC-4** - *(control: no closed balloon)* Given two regions on a
  screentone background with no outline, or inside an outline that is open
  to the page edge, when merged, then both come back unchanged. The flood
  touching the page edge, or exceeding `BALLOON_MAX_PAGE_FRACTION` of the page,
  means there is no balloon.
- **AC-5** - Given a `box` region and a `bubble` region inside one closed
  outline, when merged, then they merge, and the merged `kind` is the kind of
  the member with the most mask pixels. (A narration box's text can be
  `kind: box`.)
- **AC-6** - Given `detect_page_regions` with a fake session whose `seg` and
  `blk` produce AC-1's three regions, when it runs, then it returns one
  region. With balloon merging removed from the composition, the same call
  returns three.

## Contract

**Writes:** `src/mangatl/detect/balloons.py`, `src/mangatl/detect/page.py`, `tests/core/test_detect_balloons.py`, `tests/core/test_detect_page.py`

### C-1 Module

```python
# src/mangatl/detect/balloons.py
BALLOON_LIGHT_GREY: int            # a source pixel at or above this is paper
BALLOON_MAX_PAGE_FRACTION: float   # a flood bigger than this is page, not balloon
BALLOON_ERASE_DILATION_PX: int     # how far each region mask is painted out first

def merge_by_balloon(
    regions: Sequence[RawRegion], page: NDArray[np.uint8]   # (H, W, 3) RGB
) -> list[RawRegion]:
```

1. grey = luma of `page`. Every region's mask, dilated by
   `BALLOON_ERASE_DILATION_PX`, is set to 255, so the glyphs stop dividing
   the paper.
2. light = grey >= `BALLOON_LIGHT_GREY`. Take its 4-connected components.
3. A region's balloon is the component holding the most of its painted-out
   pixels. The component is **closed** if it touches no page edge and its area
   is at most `BALLOON_MAX_PAGE_FRACTION x H x W`. Otherwise the region has no
   balloon.
4. Regions sharing one closed balloon merge: the union of their masks,
   with the polygon as the union's bounding ring (the `columns._merge`
   convention), and `confidence` weighted by each member's pixel count.
   `merged_from` is the members' positions in **this function's input**, and
   `kind` follows AC-5. The union of masks is rebuilt with
   `postprocess._encode_one_bit_png`, imported, not re-implemented (the
   precedent is `columns.py`).
5. A region with no balloon, or alone in its balloon, is returned as given.
6. The output is sorted by mask-extent `(y0, x0)`, like `merge_columns`.

### C-2 Composition

`detect/page.py`:
`merge_by_balloon(merge_columns(regions, fences), page)`. It runs after the
column merge, so `merged_from` on a balloon merge indexes `merge_columns`'
output. That is consistent with `RawRegion`'s "positions in the sequence that
was merged".

### C-3 Constants: measured, never read off the benchmark

`BALLOON_LIGHT_GREY`, `BALLOON_MAX_PAGE_FRACTION` and
`BALLOON_ERASE_DILATION_PX` are derived in GREEN on the **MT-143
development set**: the balloons it confirms as one region, and the
neighbouring balloons it confirms as separate. Each constant's comment cites
the measured band and the value chosen inside it (the `columns.py`
precedent). Benchmark pages 3-58 are never opened for this.

### C-4 Oracle partition

AC-1 to AC-6 are **mechanical** on synthetic pages. The constants are
**oracle-free**: RED writes AC-3 and AC-4 so that they hold at *any* value of
the constants inside a plausible band (paper 200-250, max fraction
0.05-0.5). They must not tune to one value.

### C-5 Callers

`detect_page_regions`' signature is unchanged. `merge_columns` is unchanged
(MT-145 owns its rule). Only the call inside `page.py` changes.
