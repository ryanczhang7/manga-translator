## Context

EPIC-13 priority 4. On the benchmark's p023 the cleaned page shows
half-erased `合計1433点` and `14330円`. Their glyphs cross the edge of the
detector's fence box, and the erase mask is `seg ∩ box`, dilated by 2 px
(`clean/mask.py`), so each glyph is erased only up to that edge. The same
mechanism can leave the stroke ends that the `seg` head under-fires on. Six
measured benchmark regions sit between 0.0147 and 0.0355 residual, against a
0.0100 limit. Their crops are **not yet** attributed to a cause, and MT-143
looks at them on the development set first.

A glyph on paper is a dark connected component surrounded by light pixels.
When the mask already covers part of one, the rest of it is Japanese too. So
the erase mask grows to every such component it touches, on **paper only**.
A dark component whose surroundings are not light is art or screentone and
is never grown into. Neither is a component larger than a glyph, such as a
balloon's outline.

`pipeline/bake.py` recomputes `erase_mask` to know which pixels come from the
cleaned page and which restore the source for an excluded region (MT-119
C-3). If clean and bake disagree on the mask, the grown pixels show the
Japanese again in the baked page. So the page is an argument, and both sides
compute the **same** mask from the same source scan.

Required gates: `unit`, `coverage`.

## Acceptance criteria

On synthetic pages (white paper, black glyph blobs, a dark balloon outline,
a screentone patch):

- **AC-1** - Given a region whose mask covers the top 60 % of a glyph
  blob, when `erase_mask(regions, size, page)` runs, then the whole blob, plus
  `MASK_DILATION_PX`, is in the mask.
- **AC-2** - *(control: the outline)* Given a region whose dilated mask
  touches the balloon's outline, then no outline pixel is added. The outline is
  one component, larger than `GLYPH_MAX_SIDE_PX`.
- **AC-3** - *(control: art)* Given a mask touching a dark blob of glyph size
  inside a screentone patch, then the blob is not added: its surrounding ring
  is under `GLYPH_PAPER_RING_FRACTION` light.
- **AC-4** - With `page=None`, the mask is exactly today's: the union of
  masks, dilated. Existing callers that pass no page are unchanged.
- **AC-5** - Given AC-1's page through `clean_page_image` and then a bake
  with the region **excluded** after the clean, then the baked page equals the
  source on the grown pixels too. Bake's restore uses the same grown mask.
- **AC-6** - Given AC-1's page baked with the region included, then no pixel
  of the grown area shows the source glyph.

## Contract

**Writes:** `src/mangatl/clean/mask.py`, `src/mangatl/clean/page.py`, `src/mangatl/pipeline/bake.py`, `tests/core/test_clean_mask.py`, `tests/core/test_clean_inpaint.py`, `tests/core/test_bake.py`

```python
GLYPH_INK_GREY: int               # a source pixel at or below this is ink
GLYPH_MAX_SIDE_PX: int            # a dark component wider or taller than this is not a glyph
GLYPH_PAPER_RING_FRACTION: float  # share of its 2 px outer ring that must be paper (>= 200)
def erase_mask(
    regions: Sequence[RawRegion],
    page_size: tuple[int, int],
    page: NDArray[np.uint8] | None = None,   # (H, W) grey source scan
) -> NDArray[np.bool_]
```

- The union is grown **before** the dilation, and the dilation is applied
  once to the grown union.
- **Callers of the changed signature** (grep at PLANNED, 2026-11-07):
  - `clean/page.py:43`;
  - `pipeline/bake.py:296`, `:326` and `:327`;
  - `tests/core/test_clean_inpaint.py:360` and `:479`;
  - `tests/core/test_clean_mask.py`.

  The new parameter is optional, so none breaks. **`page.py` and all three
  `bake.py` sites must pass the source**, or AC-5 fails. RED's handoff
  confirms the list with `rg -n "erase_mask" src tests`.
- Bake already reads the source scan for the restore. It converts the scan
  to grey once per page, the same conversion `clean/page.py` uses.
- The three constants are derived in GREEN from MT-143's development-set
  crops: glyph sizes at 1414 px, outline thickness and screentone. They are
  never read off the benchmark.
- Oracle partition: AC-1 to AC-6 are **mechanical**. The constants are
  **oracle-free**, and AC-2 and AC-3 hold across a band of each.
