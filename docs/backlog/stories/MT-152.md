## Context

EPIC-13 priority 4. The erase mask is the detector's `seg ∩ box` pixels,
dilated by 2 px (`clean/mask.py`). A glyph that the mask covers only in part
is erased only in part, and the rest of it stays on the cleaned page.

**Where this was seen, and what may be used for what.** The user saw it on the
benchmark's p023 (half-erased `合計1433点` and `14330円`). That page is in the
held-out chapter: it is the reason the story exists and **nothing else**. No
constant, test fixture or before/after in this story reads the benchmark
chapter (pages 3-58).

**The development set does show the defect** (re-planned 2026-11-17).
MT-143's audit says "no region on either chapter is half-erased by a fence
edge under this measurement". That measurement looked only at the regions over
the residual limit and asked whether their counted ink lay inside the erase
mask. It could not see ink left *outside* a truth polygon, or a leftover too
small to cross the limit. A direct count, made at this re-plan on the
post-MT-151 development project, finds what it missed: dark components that
the mask overlaps and does not cover. Looked at as crops, the largest are
punctuation the mask clipped: the dashes after `間前` (p063) and `7月` (p064),
both brackets of a `（...）` aside (p067), a stroke of `ー` (p089), the dots of
`!!` (p084), strokes of `ガチャ` (p075). The numbers and the method are in
Contract C-6. They are a planning probe, **to be re-measured, not assumed**.

A glyph on paper is a dark connected component surrounded by light pixels.
When the mask already covers part of one, the rest of it is text too. So the
erase mask grows to every such component it overlaps, on **paper only**. A
dark component whose surroundings are not light is art or screentone and is
never grown into. Neither is a component larger than a glyph, such as a
balloon's outline that a glyph touches.

`pipeline/bake.py` recomputes `erase_mask` in `_restore_excluded`, to put the
source back over a region the user excluded after the page was cleaned (MT-119
C-3). If the clean grew the mask and the restore does not, the grown pixels
stay erased under a region that is meant to be left as drawn. So the source
page is an argument, and both sides compute the **same** mask from the same
scan through one conversion.

Required gates: `unit`, `coverage` (both `required`; they run
`tests/core/test_clean_mask.py`, the suite that fails if the growth breaks),
and `integration` for this story (`required_gates`), because two integration
tests pin "the cleaner changes nothing outside the erase mask" on real pages
and only that gate runs them.

## Acceptance criteria

On synthetic pages (white paper, black glyph blobs, a dark balloon outline,
a screentone patch). "The mask" of a region means its own stored mask, before
any dilation.

- **AC-1** - Given a region whose mask covers the top 60 % of a glyph
  blob, when `erase_mask(regions, size, page)` runs, then the result is
  exactly the region's mask united with the whole blob, dilated by
  `MASK_DILATION_PX`: the whole blob is in it, and nothing else is added.
- **AC-2** - *(control: the outline)* Given a region whose mask overlaps a
  balloon's outline, when `erase_mask(regions, size, page)` runs, then the
  result equals `erase_mask(regions, size)`: no outline pixel is added. The
  outline is one component, wider or taller than `GLYPH_MAX_SIDE_PX`.
  *Control:* the same page and mask with `GLYPH_MAX_SIDE_PX` raised above the
  outline's longer side **does** add the outline, so the size rule is what
  held it back.
- **AC-3** - *(control: art)* Given a region whose mask overlaps a dark blob
  of glyph size inside a screentone patch, then the result equals
  `erase_mask(regions, size)`: the blob is not added, because under
  `GLYPH_PAPER_RING_FRACTION` of its surrounding ring is paper.
  *Control:* the same blob and mask on white paper **is** added whole.
- **AC-4** - With `page=None`, or with no `page` argument, the mask is exactly
  today's: the union of masks, dilated. Existing callers that pass no page are
  unchanged.
- **AC-5** - Given AC-1's page cleaned through `clean_page_image`, and the
  region then **excluded**, when the chapter is baked, then the baked page
  equals the source on every pixel of the grown mask, the grown part of the
  blob included. Bake's restore uses the same grown mask.
- **AC-6** - Given AC-1's page cleaned through `clean_page_image` and baked
  with the region **included**, then on the grown pixels the baked page equals
  the cleaned page, not the source: no part of the blob is put back.
- **AC-7** - Given a `page` that is not a `(height, width)` `uint8` array for
  the `(width, height)` page size, when `erase_mask` runs, then it raises
  `ValueError` naming both sizes (or the dtype). A page is never resized,
  transposed, cropped or cast silently.

### C-1 Names and signatures (`src/mangatl/clean/mask.py`)

```python
GLYPH_INK_GREY: int               # a source pixel at or below this is ink
GLYPH_MAX_SIDE_PX: int            # a component wider OR taller than this is not a glyph
GLYPH_RING_PX: int                # radius of the ring looked at around a component
GLYPH_PAPER_GREY: int             # a ring pixel at or above this is paper
GLYPH_PAPER_RING_FRACTION: float  # share of the ring that must be paper

def source_grey(rgb: NDArray[np.uint8]) -> NDArray[np.uint8]: ...

def erase_mask(
    regions: Sequence[RawRegion],
    page_size: tuple[int, int],
    page: NDArray[np.uint8] | None = None,   # source_grey(...) of the source scan
) -> NDArray[np.bool_]: ...
```

- All five constants, and `source_grey`, are added to `__all__`.
  `MASK_DILATION_PX` is unchanged.
- **The constants are read at call time, never bound at import**, as
  `MASK_DILATION_PX` already is. Tests set them with `monkeypatch` and never
  depend on the shipped values (C-6 says why).
- `erase_mask` keeps its return: a `(height, width)` boolean array. It still
  raises `ValueError` for a mask that is not page-sized.

### C-2 The one grey conversion

`source_grey(rgb)` takes the decoded source as an `(H, W, 3)` RGB `uint8`
array and returns `np.asarray(Image.fromarray(rgb, "RGB").convert("L"))`: PIL's
ITU-R 601-2 luma, `(H, W)` `uint8`. It is the **only** place a source scan
becomes grey for the mask.

- `clean/page.py` already holds that array: `_decoded_page(image_bytes)`.
- `pipeline/bake.py` already holds it too: `scan` in `_bake`, passed to
  `_restore_excluded` as `source`. Both decode with `image.convert("RGB")`.
- Neither module converts to grey any other way for this purpose. Bake's
  `_grey(cleaned)` reads the **cleaned** page for `bubble_interior` and is
  not touched.
- For a greyscale scan the round trip is exact: PIL's L of `(g, g, g)` is
  `g`.

### C-3 What is grown

With `page` given, and `union` the union of the regions' decoded masks
(before dilation):

1. `ink = page <= GLYPH_INK_GREY`, over the **whole page**. The mask is not
   subtracted first.
2. The **8-connected** components of `ink`.
3. A component is **touched** when at least one of its pixels is in `union`.
   The test is against the raw union, never the dilated mask.
4. A touched component is a **glyph** when both hold:
   - its bounding box is at most `GLYPH_MAX_SIDE_PX` wide **and** at most
     `GLYPH_MAX_SIDE_PX` tall (equal to the limit is a glyph);
   - its **ring** is at least `GLYPH_PAPER_RING_FRACTION` paper (equal to the
     limit is a glyph).
5. The **ring** of a component is every pixel within `GLYPH_RING_PX` of it
   that is not in it and is on the page: the component dilated by the
   elliptical kernel of side `2 x GLYPH_RING_PX + 1` (the shape
   `MASK_DILATION_PX` uses), minus the component. Pixels off the page are
   not counted on either side of the fraction. A ring pixel is **paper** when
   `page >= GLYPH_PAPER_GREY`. Other ink in the ring counts against the
   fraction. An empty ring is not paper: the component is not a glyph.
6. `grown = union | every glyph component, whole`.
7. The result is `grown` dilated **once** by `MASK_DILATION_PX`, as today.
   With radius 0 it is `grown` itself.

With `page=None` steps 1 to 6 are skipped and `grown = union` (AC-4).

**Growth distributes over union**, because whether a component is a glyph
depends on the page alone and not on the mask: `erase_mask(A + B, size, page)
== erase_mask(A, size, page) | erase_mask(B, size, page)`. `_restore_excluded`
relies on this, as it relied on dilation distributing (MT-019 C-3). A design
that subtracts the mask before labelling, or tests the ring against the mask,
breaks it. RED pins it with a test.

Growth happens once. A component is not grown because a *grown* component
touches it.

### C-4 The page argument's shape (AC-7)

`page` must have `page.shape == (height, width)` and `page.dtype == np.uint8`.
Anything else raises `ValueError` before any mask is decoded: a three-channel
array, a transposed page, a page of another size, a float or boolean array.
The message names the page's shape and the expected `width x height`, or the
dtype.

### C-8 What does not change

- `clean_page`, the inpainter and `MASK_DILATION_PX`.
- A page cleaned before this story keeps its stored cleaned image. Baking it
  is safe: the restore pastes source over grown pixels that were never
  erased, which changes nothing.
- Exclusion is not consulted by the growth. `CleanStage` hands the cleaner
  only included regions, so a glyph of an excluded region is erased only if
  an included region's mask overlaps that same component.
