## Context

EPIC-13 priority 3. The user decided this on 2026-11-07 (U-3): the bench
gains a check for p017's slip, reported beside S1b and not part of it. In
copy A, the slip left two neighbouring pairs of regions with **identical
English from different Japanese**: regions 4/5 ("WEE" for `っ` and
`いてーよー！！`) and 6/7 ("It hurts!!" for `いてーよー！！` and the battlefield
line). The pattern is cheap to count and needs no truth. It does not see a
slip that produces different English, so it is a floor, and the report says
so.

S1b did not move in MT-108. The reviewer accepted both copies' lines
unedited, so a slip is invisible to the headline (MT-108 "What S1b does and
does not say"). That is why it is its own line.

Required gates: `unit`, `coverage`.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** - Given a page with two regions that are neighbours in reading
  order (both included, both proposed), reading indices i and j, whose
  `normalise(proposed_en)` are equal and non-empty and whose
  `normalise(source_ja)` differ, when `count_slips(project)` runs, then it
  reports that pair as `(ordinal, i, j)`.
- **AC-2** - *(control: repeated dialogue is not a slip)* Two neighbours
  with identical Japanese and identical English (copy B's
  `いてーよー！！` → "It huuurts!!" twice) are **not** reported.
- **AC-3** - *(control: neighbours only)* Equal English from different
  Japanese two regions apart, or across a page break, is not reported.
- **AC-4** - Excluded regions and regions with no proposal are skipped. The
  pair is formed over the remaining regions in reading order, so a skipped
  region between two equal neighbours makes them neighbours, and the pair
  carries both regions' own reading indices (regions 4 and 6 are `4/6`).
- **AC-5** - `python -m mangatl.bench` prints, after the acceptance block
  (the five lines S1b, S1a, recall, spurious, identity) and before S2,
  `slips (identical English, different Japanese)  N`. When N > 0, the next
  line lists `page P regions i/j` for each pair, joined by `, `. Neither
  changes S1b, S1a or S2's figures or verdicts.
- **AC-6** - It reads `proposed_en`, the pipeline's output, never
  `final_en`: a slip the reviewer fixed by hand is still the pipeline's slip.

**C-1 The module.**

```python
# bench/slip.py
@dataclass(frozen=True)
class SlipReport:
    pairs: tuple[tuple[int, int, int], ...]   # (page ordinal, first reading index, second reading index)
def count_slips(project: Project) -> SlipReport
def render(report: SlipReport) -> list[str]
```

- `__all__ = ["SlipReport", "count_slips", "render"]`.
- `normalise` is `domain.line.normalise` (O2's comparison form), imported. It
  is applied to both `proposed_en` and `source_ja`.
- `count_slips` reads every page of `project.pages()`, in ordinal order, with
  *(RED amendment, 2026-11-16: `Project.pages` is a method, not a property -
  `store/project.py` line 883, "A query, not a cached tuple" - so the call is
  `project.pages()`; iterating `project.pages` raises `TypeError: 'method'
  object is not iterable`. Meaning unchanged.)*
  `store.lines.read_review_lines(project, ordinal)`. That gives one entry per
  region in reading order. An entry is skipped when it is `None`, when
  `line.excluded is not None`, when `line.proposed_en is None`, or when
  `line.misaligned` is true. *(Amended in RED, 2026-11-16, user decision D-3:
  the block said a misaligned region "has no proposal", but
  `Project.write_proposed` keeps a misaligned region's earlier `proposed_en`
  (`store/project.py:1212-1215`). Its leftover English comes from an older
  run, and this run's answer for it was already dropped by MT-149's guard,
  so it is skipped explicitly.)* The remaining lines are compared pairwise, each with its successor, within
  one page only.
- A pair is a slip when `normalise(a.proposed_en) == normalise(b.proposed_en)`,
  that value is not `""`, and `normalise(a.source_ja) != normalise(b.source_ja)`.
  Two regions with empty Japanese (`ocr_empty`) compare equal, so they are
  not a slip.
- `pairs` is ordered by page ordinal, then first reading index. The indices
  are `Line.reading_index` as stored, and the ordinal is `Page.ordinal` as
  stored. Nothing is renumbered for display. Three regions in a row with the
  same English and three different Japanese are two pairs, `(p, 0, 1)` and
  `(p, 1, 2)`.
- It reads `proposed_en` only (AC-6). `final_en` and `status` are not read.
- Needs no truth and no `development_use` check, and raises nothing of its own.

**C-2 The rendered lines.** `render` returns exactly:

```
slips (identical English, different Japanese)  0
```

for no pairs (one line, two spaces before the count), and for N pairs two
lines:

```
slips (identical English, different Japanese)  2
page 17 regions 4/5, page 17 regions 6/7
```

Every pair is on the one second line, in `pairs` order, joined by `, `.
