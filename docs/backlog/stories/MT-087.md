## Context

Filed 2026-10-14 when MT-048 was narrowed (MT-048 PO-1, the user's decision).
`layout.md` §"Review workspace" sizes the strip as "96px fixed (72px thumbnail +
`space.3` either side)" and the ≥1440 breakpoint row says "full 96px with
thumbnails"; `components.md` §`PageStrip` is "a vertical list of page
thumbnails". After MT-048 the 96 px strip still shows filenames. `RunThumbnail`
(`ui/run_thumbnail.py`, MT-062) already decodes and scales a page for the run
screen at 120 × 160 and notes that 160 px ≈ 2.2× the strip's 72 px — the scaling
is worth sharing, the widget is not (it is non-interactive and paints markers).

## Acceptance criteria

*Draft; to be pinned at PLANNED → RED against `components.md` §`PageStrip` and
`layout.md`.*

- **AC-1** — Given a chapter of decodable pages, when the strip is at 96 px,
  then each item shows a 72 px-wide thumbnail of its page, aspect preserved,
  decoded once per page and cached for the life of the chapter.
- **AC-2** — Given a page whose bytes do not decode, when the strip is laid out,
  then its item shows the "skipped" placeholder from `components.md` and the
  strip remains usable (MT-015 AC-6's behaviour holds).
- **AC-3** — Given the window drops below 1440, when the rail is shown, then no
  thumbnail is painted or decoded for the rail; crossing back up shows the
  cached thumbnails without re-decoding.
- **AC-4** — Decoding 20 pages for the strip takes under a bound RED measures
  and pins (with a negative control), so opening a chapter does not stall the
  window.

## Contract

To be pinned. Expected shape: a `QListWidget.IconMode`-free approach — a
per-item `QIcon`/`QPixmap` at 72 px via `Qt.DecorationRole`, sized by
`setIconSize`, with the filename as tooltip/accessible text; decode through the
same `QImageReader` path `RunThumbnail.show_page` uses, factored into a helper
both can call.

## Out of scope

Status glyphs (MT-086). Remembering a collapsed strip (MT-088).
