## Context

Brief success criterion **S2**: "every text-bearing bubble on the page is found,
and no Japanese pixels survive inside a cleaned region. Measured separately from
S1 — a perfect translation in a missed bubble is still a failure, and a leftover
glyph is visible at a glance."

Two measurements, deliberately in one story because they share a ground truth
and because reporting one without the other reproduces the blindness the S1
metrics have — S1b, the headline, cannot see a spurious detection at all, and
**this story is where spurious detections are a reported failure** (AC-3).
`docs/wiki/stack.md` §5/O2 says so explicitly and points here:

- **Recall** — fraction of ground-truth text regions matched by a detection.
- **Residual ink** — whether Japanese pixels survive inside a cleaned region.

The second is the harder one to measure honestly, and it is where a blind metric
is easy to write: "mean absolute difference from the source is high inside the
region" is satisfied by a cleaner that paints the region bright pink.

**Which required gate would fail if this story's artifact broke:** `unit` and
`coverage-core` — `bench` is in the 100% set.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** — Given a chapter and its ground truth, when recall is measured, then
  it reports matched, missed and spurious region counts, matching by mask IoU ≥
  0.5, and the per-page breakdown.
- **AC-2** — Given a ground-truth region that no detection matches, when recall
  is measured, then it is counted as **missed** and the page it is on is named.
- **AC-3** — Given a detection matching no ground-truth region, when recall is
  measured, then it is counted as **spurious**. *(A detector that emits a
  thousand boxes must not score 100% recall unremarked.)*
- **AC-4** — Given a cleaned page and its source, when residual ink is measured,
  then it reports, per region, the fraction of pixels that remain "ink-like" —
  dark, high-local-contrast — inside the cleaned mask.
- **AC-5** — *(negative control: the metric sees surviving glyphs)* Given a page
  where cleaning was **skipped entirely**, when residual ink is measured, then
  the score exceeds `RESIDUAL_INK_MAX` by a wide margin. Record the two numbers.
- **AC-6** — *(negative control: the metric is about ink, not about difference)*
  Given a page where each region was **filled with a flat mid-grey**, when
  residual ink is measured, then it scores **below** the threshold — the metric
  must not reward "different from the source", only "no ink left".
  *(The complementary check — that a flat fill is nonetheless a bad clean — is
  MT-019 AC-7's tone-variance criterion, deliberately in the other story.)*
- **AC-7** — Given a chapter marked as used during development, when the
  measurement runs in benchmark mode, then it refuses, as MT-022 AC-8 does.
- **AC-8** — Given both measurements, when the report is produced, then S2 is
  reported as a **pair** — recall and residual ink — and passing one while
  failing the other is reported as a failure, not as a partial pass.

    INK_CONTRAST_MIN: float

src/mangatl/bench/s2.py

**Semantics behind each number — and the trap this story exists to avoid:**

- **"Ink-like" is a two-condition test, not a brightness test**: a pixel is ink
  if its luma is below `INK_LUMA_MAX` **and** its local contrast against a small
  neighbourhood exceeds `INK_CONTRAST_MIN`. Brightness alone counts dense
  screentone and dark artwork as surviving Japanese, which makes the metric
  fire on correctly cleaned pages and get quietly relaxed until it fires on
  nothing.
- **Local contrast is what distinguishes a glyph from a dark region.** A glyph
  is a high-frequency structure; a shaded area is not. This is the same
  one-point-versus-two-point distinction `story-authoring` records for the
  variance-versus-gradient case, and it is why AC-6 exists: a flat mid-grey fill
  is dark-ish and has near-zero local contrast, so an ink metric must score it
  low while a difference metric scores it high.
- **`RESIDUAL_INK_MAX`** is **not settled here.** GREEN measures it on the
  fixture set, records the skipped-cleaning control value alongside it, and sets
  the threshold with stated headroom below the control and above the clean runs.
- **Spurious detections are counted and reported but do not reduce recall**
  (AC-3). They are a different failure — a phantom bubble — and MT-008 owns the
  main cause of them. Reporting them here is what stops recall being gamed.

**Oracle partition:**

| AC | Kind | Instruction to RED |
|---|---|---|
| AC-1..AC-3, AC-7, AC-8 | **Mechanical** | Constructed detections and truth. Exact counts. |
| AC-4..AC-6 | **Oracle-free** | The ink metric has no settled definition. **Invent it, and both controls are mandatory.** AC-6 is the one that distinguishes an ink metric from a difference metric, and it is the one a plausible implementation fails. |

## Deferred verifications

- *Falsifiable condition 1:* with `ink_fraction` reduced to a **luma-only** test
  (drop the contrast condition), AC-6 **must** fail — a flat mid-grey fill would
  score as ink.
- *Falsifiable condition 2:* with `ink_fraction` replaced by **mean absolute
  difference from the source**, AC-6 **must** fail. This is the single most
  valuable mutation in the story: it is the plausible wrong metric, and AC-5
  alone does not catch it.
- *Falsifiable condition 3:* with `IOU_MATCH_THRESHOLD` set to `0.0` — a wrong
  value — AC-2 and AC-3 **must** fail (everything matches everything).
- *Why RED cannot run these:* no implementation exists; the controls' expected
  values are claims in RED and are confirmed by GREEN against the shipped module.
- **Owner: GATES.** Three mutations via `bash scripts/mutate.sh`.

## Model guidance

- **RED and GREEN models:** to be recorded at dispatch, by name. Defaults are
  `claude-opus-5` (Claude Opus 5).
- **Oracle partition mixed**; the table goes in the dispatch prompt verbatim.
  The instruction that carries the story is on AC-4–AC-6: *invent the metric, and
  demand a negative control that fires hard* — with the explicit warning that a
  difference-from-source metric passes AC-5 and fails the product, and that AC-6
  is what separates them.
- **Success condition, falsifiable:** the separation between the
  skipped-cleaning control and the clean runs is at least an order of magnitude,
  and the flat-fill control scores **below** the threshold rather than merely
  lower than the skipped one. Both numbers in the handoff. A separation narrow
  enough that fixture noise decides it sends the criterion back to the PO.
- **Verdict:** *to be recorded.*
